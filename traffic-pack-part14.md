# Traffic Pack — Part 14, "How Banks Take Your Money Without Touching It"

Ready-to-paste distribution for https://youtube.com/shorts/OHVnm5R0o5E.
Same structure as `traffic-pack.md` (Part 12), narrowed to the two platforms
being tried first. The other levers in that file — Community poll, Reddit,
WhatsApp/Telegram — still apply and are worth running after these two.

Everything here is organic. The warning at the top of `traffic-pack.md` stands:
never buy views or engagement. On a channel this size one bot wave ends
distribution permanently.

**The single most important mechanic on both platforms: upload the file
natively. Never post a link to the YouTube video.** Both algorithms suppress
posts that send users off-platform, and a link post gets a fraction of the reach
a native video does. The video is already 1080×1920 with burned-in captions, so
it needs no re-export for either.

## TikTok

Largest cold-reach engine for finance explainers, and the burned-in captions are
a real advantage here — most TikTok viewing starts sound-off, and a video that
reads silently keeps people past the first second.

**Caption** (the first ~70 characters are all that show before "more", so the
hook is front-loaded):

```
Someone takes money out of your pocket every single day. No receipt. No alert. And it's completely legal.

Money isn't printed — it's LENT into existence. A bank approves a loan and the money simply appears. It was typed into a screen, not taken from a vault.

Every loan creates new money. But the world's bread didn't multiply. More money, same bread — so bread costs more.

That's all inflation is.

#inflation #costofliving #moneytok #fintok #personalfinance #islamicfinance #halalmoney #muslimmoney #soundmoney #financetok
```

**Platform notes:**

- **No link in the caption.** TikTok demotes captions containing URLs. Put the
  YouTube link in the bio and say "full series in bio" only if it feels natural.
- **Hashtag mix is deliberate:** broad discovery tags first (`#inflation`,
  `#costofliving`, `#moneytok`, `#fintok`) because that is where cold reach
  lives, then the niche cluster (`#islamicfinance`, `#halalmoney`,
  `#muslimmoney`) to find the warm audience. This mirrors what works on the
  channel — the mechanism is the answer, not the hook.
- **Post natively at full length.** 1:40 is well inside TikTok's limit, and
  longer watch time on a completed view is a stronger signal than a trim.
- **First comment:** drop the engagement question there rather than in the
  caption, so the caption stays about the hook:
  > Your wage went up this year. Did it actually go further? 👇

## One caveat on reading the numbers

This Short went out at 06:02 PT Sunday, outside the channel's proven
03:00–04:30 PT window. Cross-posting to TikTok and X adds two more uncontrolled
variables on top of that. If Part 14 outperforms, it will not be clear whether
that was the new production style, the title, the off-window publish, or the
cross-posting — so treat this launch as exploratory rather than as a clean test
of any one of them. Changing one variable per upload is what makes the next
decision informative.
