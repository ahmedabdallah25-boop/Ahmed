EPIDEMIC SOUND — HOW TO USE THE AUDIO TAGS
================================================================================

Every scene below carries a line like:

  🎵 mood: Dark + Suspense · genre: Cinematic / Drone · energy 2 · 60–70 BPM
     search: "sparse cinematic drone tension unresolved"

`mood:` values are taken from **Epidemic Sound's own mood vocabulary** so you can
type them straight into the mood filter rather than translating: Angry, Dark,
Dreamy, Elegant, Epic, Euphoric, Floating, Happy, Heavy & Ponderous, Hopeful,
Laid Back, Mysterious, Peaceful, Restless, Running, Sad, Scary, Sentimental,
Smooth, Sneaking, Suspense, Weird.

`energy` is 1–10 on their energy slider. `search:` is a phrase to paste into the
prompt box if you are generating rather than browsing.

**The arc matters more than any single cue.** Do not score this scene by scene with
twelve different tracks — that is the single most common way an explainer starts
feeling like a trailer. Pick **three beds** and let them run:

  BED A · Scenes 01–09    Dark, Mysterious, Suspense · energy 2–3 · unresolved
  BED B · Scenes 10–22    Sentimental, Hopeful, Floating · energy 3–5 · warming
  BED C · Scenes 23–30    Hopeful, Peaceful, Epic (restrained) · energy 4–6 · resolves

The per-scene tags then tell you **where inside each bed to sit** — where to drop
to nothing, where to let it build, where the one hit lands (Scene 27).

ONE HARD RULE ON MUSIC: cut it entirely under every recitation and under every
Arabic word study. Scenes 04, 11, 12, 19, 25 are marked `MUSIC: OUT`. A bed under
recitation is the fastest way to lose the exact viewer this channel is for.

IF YOU USE NO INSTRUMENTS: some creators in this niche don't. Every cue below works
as sound design instead — Epidemic's ambience and drone libraries cover it. Swap
`genre: Cinematic` for `genre: Ambience / Drone` and search the same mood words;
the arc is unchanged. Room tone, paper, a distant call, wind. Your call, not mine.
