# The Deep Made Simple formula, reverse-engineered

**Reference:** [@deepmadesimple](https://www.youtube.com/@deepmadesimple) · `UCiVywgvam7BPUwJ-zwxuRGg`
256,000 subscribers · 13,874,493 views · 193 uploads · joined 28 Feb 2026 · **98% faceless**
(Nexlev classifier, confidence 0.98) · **sells nothing** — 0 products, 0 sponsors, 0 store URLs.

That last line matters before anything else. There is no funnel, no course, no sponsor money
buying reach. Whatever is driving 13.9M views in six months is in the videos.

Clarity in the Quran is a transposition of this channel — same description with the nouns swapped,
several titles swapped one-for-one. So the question is not "what should we copy", it is **"we
already copied the packaging; what did we not copy?"**

---

## The answer in one line

**Deep Made Simple is a lecture with slides. Clarity is a poem with pictures.**

Its images are information graphics that carry the argument — a timeline, a labelled comparison,
a chain diagram, a huge number — changing every 3–5 seconds for twenty to thirty minutes.
Clarity's images are evocative: four faint marks, three ink letterforms, two columns of marks.
Beautiful, and they explain nothing.

Everything below is the evidence for that, and the parts of it worth copying.

## 2. The topic archetype: one detail that reverses an assumption

Nexlev's outlier pass over the 100 most recent uploads returns **six** videos above 2× the channel
average of 71,889. Every single one is the same shape:

| Outlier | Views | Runtime | The shape |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| The Gospel of John: The One Word He Used 98 Times (And the One He Refused To) | 364K | 31:56 | **5.1×** — one word, counted |
| What "Honor Your Parents" Actually Means in Hebrew | 312K | 27:51 | **4.3×** — original-language meaning |
| Why Jesus Cursed the Fig Tree, The Detail in Mark 11 That Changes the Whole Story | 211K | 19:35 | **2.9×** — one detail |
| What "The Word" Really Means in John 1:1 | 208K | 22:22 | **2.9×** — one phrase, one verse |
| The Parable of the Talents: It Was Never About Your Gifts | 157K | 30:45 | **2.2×** — flat reversal |
| The Hebrew Word for "Glory" Has Nothing to Do with Light | 153K | 31:35 | **2.1×** — word + reversal |

Not one "Every X Explained" among them. The formula is:

> **[One specific word, phrase, or textual detail] + [does not mean what you assume] + [the
> original-language / textual reason why]**

The claim must be *falsifiable and precise*. "What the Quran says about anger" is a topic.
"The Arabic word for anger in 3:134 is not the word you were taught" is a video.

**Caveat, stated honestly.** Across all time the "Every X Explained" listicles are the channel's
biggest raw numbers — *Every Letter the Apostle Paul Wrote* is its 1.1M all-time top. Those are
April uploads and fall outside the outlier tool's 100-video window. So the fair reading is: the
listicles built the channel, the one-detail reversals are what is compounding **now**. Both work.
Clarity's own best video, *Every Minor Prophet Explained* (193), is a listicle.

## 3. The hook: a hard number as the literal first word

`4ykVQrLLIjs`, the 5.1× outlier, opens:

> **"90%.** That is roughly how much of John's gospel you will not find in Matthew, Mark, or Luke.
> Same Jesus, same 3 years, same death, same resurrection, and yet roughly 90% of the material
> John put on the page exists only in his account. No birth story, no manger, no shepherds, no
> Sermon on the Mount, no parables, no exorcisms, no account of the bread and the wine at the Last
> Supper. […] **The question is why. And the answer changes how you read every chapter he wrote.**"

Five moves in ninety seconds:

1. **A number, first word.** Not a sentence containing a number — the number *is* the opening.
2. **The anomaly** it implies, stated plainly.
3. **A rapid enumeration of absences** — seven "no …" items in one breath. Concrete, countable.
4. **"The question is why."** The question asked out loud, not implied.
5. **The stakes**: "changes how you read every chapter he wrote."

No intro animation, no logo, no "welcome back", no "before we start". The first frame is content.

**Clarity is already good at this and should not change it.** Its own twin, `PUPdFpvEA04`, opens:

> "There is an instruction in the Quran that refuses to appear by itself. Go looking for it and you
> will find it in **five separate places in five different surahs** […] Nothing else gets that
> position. Not prayer, not fasting, not charity, not pilgrimage, not truthfulness, not courage."

That is the same machine — anomaly, count, enumeration of what does *not* qualify — and the prose
is arguably better. **Clarity's scripts are not the problem.** The only adjustment worth making is
moving the number to the front: lead on *"Five."*, then the sentence.

## 4. Credibility: named sources and admitted uncertainty

Inside the first ninety seconds the John video names Irenaeus, Polycarp, Ephesus, and a date range
of AD 85–95 — and then says *"though some have argued for an earlier date."*

That hedge is doing real work. Admitting a scholarly disagreement is the cheapest available signal
that a human checked the material. Clarity's most-watched video carries a public **"AI BE AWARE"**
comment; the diagnosis traced its Christian-register leakage to this very template. Naming
authorities and conceding disputes is the direct antidote, and it costs one sentence.

Standing rules already established for Clarity, unchanged and reinforced by this: verse citations
by surah and ayah, the verified-mushaf note, and the no-ruling disclaimer.

## 5. The visuals — this is the finding

Multimodal pass over the first 100 seconds of the 5.1× outlier:

- **No presenter, no face, no stock footage of people.** Two stylised illustrations of hands and a
  figure from behind; that is all the human presence there is.
- **Digital watercolour / painterly**, warm and earthy — tans, browns, yellows, with blue and
  orange accents. Not photoreal.
- **The image changes every 3–5 seconds**, with subtle Ken Burns push and drift throughout. Never
  a locked-off still.
- **No channel logo, no intro animation, no branding** in the opening.
- **Text is baked into the illustrations, and it is doing the explaining.** Observed in 100
  seconds: a large **"90%"** graphic; labels naming books; a comparison with the headers
  **"Familiar Items"** and **"New Focus"**; a **chain diagram** carrying the names
  Irenaeus → Polycarp → John; a **timeline with dates**.

That last point is the one to copy. Those are not decorations of the narration, they *are* the
argument, rendered. A viewer who mutes the video still learns something. That is what makes
twenty-seven minutes survivable without a face on screen — and it is exactly what an
assumption-reversing claim needs, because a reversal has to be *shown* against the thing it
reverses.

Clarity's art direction is already in the same family — "minimalist ink & watercolour sketch on
parchment". The style is right. **The information content of the frames is not.**

### The five graphic types to steal

| Type | Deep Made Simple | The Clarity equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **The big number** | "90%" filling the frame | "5" for the five surahs; "17" for a word's occurrence count |
| **Labelled comparison** | "Familiar Items" / "New Focus" | the word you assume / the word actually used |
| **Chain diagram** | Irenaeus → Polycarp → John | an isnad; or a word's root → derived forms |
| **Timeline with dates** | AD 70, AD 85–95 | order of revelation, Meccan vs Medinan |
| **Labelled object** | a scroll, a manger, named | a labelled mushaf page, letters called out |

### What Clarity's frames look like today

From the diagnosis's own audit of the evil-eye pack: *"three ink letterforms"*, *"four faint
marks"*, *"two columns of marks"*, *"four marks in a row"*. That audit correctly caught these as
monotonous and made four of them **concrete** — four stones in a wall, a folded cloth, an open
palm. Concrete is an improvement on abstract. **Neither is informative.** A stone in a wall does
not teach; a labelled diagram of where the four refuges sit does.

## 6. What is already right, and must not be "fixed"

- **Visual density is already solved in the plan.** The evil-eye pack independently derived the
  3–6 second rule and reaches ~5.9s per visual event via the line-art → wash cross-dissolve, at
  the same 30 generations. That reveal technique is native to the aesthetic and is a signature the
  channel can own. Keep it.
- **Long-form only.** The reference has no Shorts tab at all. Confirmed, not a constraint.
- **No banner.** The reference has none at 256K subs.
- **The prose.** See §3.

## 7. The confound I cannot remove

Deep Made Simple published **193 videos in 175 days — about 1.1 per day.** Clarity has published
18 in the ~110 days since it started, including a 55-day blackout.

I cannot separate "the format works" from "the format was run 193 times". Retention data, which
would settle it, is paywalled on both channels. So the honest ranking is:

1. **Runtime + informative visuals** — the largest lever I can evidence, and the one under your
   control per-video.
2. **Cadence** — plausibly just as large, and not something a script can fix.

Anyone who tells you which of those two matters more is guessing. Do both.

## The build spec

For the next Clarity long-form:

- [ ] **19–28 minutes.** Not padding: a bigger argument. One verse or one word, taken all the way
      down — occurrences, root, the assumption, the reversal, the consequence.
- [ ] **Topic is a falsifiable claim about one word or one detail**, not a subject area.
- [ ] **First word of the VO is a number**, and that number is the first frame as a full-bleed
      graphic.
- [ ] **At least one of each**: big number, labelled comparison, chain diagram, timeline.
      Budget roughly one information graphic every 90 seconds; the rest can be mood art.
- [ ] **A visual event every 3–6 seconds** via the line→wash reveal already specified.
- [ ] **Name two authorities and concede one disagreement.**
- [ ] **No intro, no logo, no "assalamu alaikum and welcome back".** Frame one is the number.
- [ ] Verse citations by surah and ayah; verified-mushaf note; no-ruling disclaimer.
