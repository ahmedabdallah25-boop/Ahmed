# Clarity in the Quran, benchmarked against Deep Made Simple and MrBeast

**Pulled live 2026-09-15** via Nexlev public listing tools (`youtube_channel_about`,
`youtube_channel_videos` sorted oldest): Clarity `UC0eBu0ZXcF20pTAG3lUnPXA`, Deep Made Simple
`UCiVywgvam7BPUwJ-zwxuRGg`, MrBeast `UCX6OQ3DkcsbYNE6H8uQQuVA`. Nexlev is on the free plan, so
there is **no CTR, retention or traffic-source data for any channel here, including our own** —
every conclusion below rests on title, runtime, publish order and lifetime views. Where that is
not enough to attribute a result, this file says so instead of guessing.

## 1. What the two reference channels actually show

**MrBeast is not usable as a performance benchmark, and the reason matters.** His 30 oldest
surviving videos now read 216K–35M views. They did not earn those at upload. They are 2012–13
Minecraft-mod and Pokémon clips of 0:29–6:03, and their counts are back-catalogue traffic
collected after the 2017 breakout. Reading them as launch performance would be reading the
*effect* of fame as its cause. Two things do transfer:

- He spent years at negligible real-time viewership before one premise broke, and he did not
  survive that by rotating formats. He repeated one thing and escalated it.
- Every early title states the whole premise in a handful of words, judgeable before the click.

Nothing else transfers: 2013 clip-length vlogging against a 2026 long-form tafsir channel is a
different platform, a different audience and a different watch-time economy.

**Deep Made Simple is the usable reference** — same genre shape, same year, and Clarity's own
copy already borrows from it. Its 30 oldest videos:

| | Deep Made Simple, first 30 | Clarity, all 23 |
|---|---|---|
| Runtime range | 4:30 – 27:58 | 4:33 – 36:23 |
| Runtime median | **≈18:55** | 8:03 |
| Views, first upload in listing | 43K | 229 |
| Views, best in set | 805K | 2,400 |
| Videos under 6 min | 2 (4K and 5.8K views — the two weakest in the set) | 5 |

Three findings, and one correction to our own notes:

1. **The entry runtime band is 17–22 minutes, not 27+.** `clarity/video-formula.md` records
   "median 27:40, 0 of top 30 under 19 minutes" — that is the reference's **top 30 by views**,
   measured later. Its **first 30 by date** run a median of about 18:55, 17 of 30 inside
   17:00–22:00. Both facts are true; the second is the one that applies to a channel starting
   out. Clarity's only hit, Ar-Ra'd 28 at 18:08, sits inside that band. Its two longest uploads,
   28:46 and 36:23, took 132 and 25 views.
2. **About a quarter of that early catalogue is a calendar-anchored search series** — eight of
   thirty are "Sabbath School Lesson N, Q1 2026" summaries, 4K–57K views each. That is demand
   that exists whether or not anyone has heard of the channel: a dated thing people look up on a
   schedule. Everything else in the set is book, story and character explainers, plus three
   emotional-problem videos (anxiety 223K, overthinking 69K, depression 52K).
3. **It did not start from zero.** Its first uploads in this listing already pulled 20K–223K.
   Whatever produced that — an existing audience, promotion, a team — copying its runtime and
   title shapes will not reproduce it. Only its *structure* is portable.

Caveat on dates: Nexlev buckets publish dates by month (every early Deep Made Simple video reads
2026-03-15 or 2026-04-15). Upload **cadence** cannot be derived from this source for either
channel, so no claim below rests on it.

## 2. Clarity measured against that

Live today: **51 subscribers, 4,805 lifetime views, 23 videos**, channel opened 2024-01-31.
(`clarity/channel-diagnosis.md` and `CLAUDE.md` still say 2,561 lifetime views — that was
August. The channel has roughly doubled since, and almost all of it is one video.)

- **One video is half the channel.** Ar-Ra'd 28 (18:08, ~25 Aug) has 2,400 views — 50% of
  everything the channel has ever done. With the anger video at 853, two uploads out of 23 carry
  68% of lifetime views.
- **Fourteen of 23 videos are under 100 views.** Channel median is 62.
- **Long-form is ahead, but not by enough to prove anything.** The six uploads of 17 minutes or
  more have a median of 127 views against 62 for the seventeen shorter ones. Same direction as
  the reference; n=6 against n=17 on one channel is not evidence of a cause.
- **The enumeration format is the weakest thing here.** The five "Every X Explained" videos —
  Minor Prophets 194, Plagues 193, Jinn 62, Shaytan 48, Women in the Quran 10 — have a median of
  62. The reference channel's early list-shaped wins are *numbered mechanisms* ("The 4 Stages of
  a Hardened Heart", 71K), not exhaustive catalogues.
- **Three anger videos compete with each other**: the 853-view original (6:17, May), a second at
  8:03 (185) and a third at 4:53 (44). The channel's second-best topic is split three ways.
- **The front door promises a series that does not exist.** The channel description advertises
  "verse-by-verse studies — Surah Al-Kahf, Al-Mulk, Ya-Sin, Ar-Ra'd, Al-Fatihah". No video title
  contains Al-Kahf, Al-Mulk, Ya-Sin or Al-Fatihah. One Ar-Ra'd video exists, and it is the hit.
- **Direct copies of the reference's winners underperformed**: "The Quran Verse That Actually
  Stops Anxiety" 131 views at 9:15 against the Bible original's 223K at 21:57; the depression
  copy 43 views at 11:07 against 52K at 15:08. The honest reading is audience size and channel
  authority, not the title wording — which is the point. Borrowed titles do not borrow reach.

## 3. What to actually run

1. **Repeat the one format that worked, three times, before changing anything else.** Ar-Ra'd 28
   is the only result the channel has. Its shape: one named verse, "Explained", and a single
   reversal — the half of the verse nobody quotes. Ship three more in that exact shape and
   measure. This is the MrBeast lesson correctly applied: 23 videos across roughly fifteen
   formats is why nothing compounds. One counter-example already exists (Al-Qalam, same shape,
   23 views), so treat this as a test to run, not a rule to trust.
2. **Hold runtime at 17–22 minutes.** The reference's own entry band, our own best video, and
   cheaper to produce than the 28–36 minute uploads that took 132 and 25 views.
3. **Start the weekly anchored series the description already promises: Surah Al-Kahf, Fridays.**
   This is the single most transferable structure from Deep Made Simple — its Sabbath School
   series is search demand on a schedule, and Al-Kahf on Friday is the same mechanism in this
   niche. The channel's own keyword list already carries "surah al kahf" and "friday". One
   verse-block per week, 17–22 minutes, numbered in the title the way the reference numbers its
   lessons, because there the number is the search term.
4. **Stop the "Every X Explained" catalogues.** Five tries, median 62. Convert the survivors to
   numbered mechanisms when they are repackaged.
5. **Repackage only the losers.** `clarity/packaging-fix.json` already protects proven winners,
   and retitling a settled video measured +0 on channel 1. So Ar-Ra'd 28 and the 853-view anger
   video stay untouched; the second and third anger videos are the ones to retitle onto distinct
   queries.
6. **Every one of these is over three minutes, so every one needs its 16:9 custom thumbnail**
   per `CLAUDE.md`, built to `clarity/thumbnail-system.md` — no photoreal AI humans, thumbnail
   text never repeats the title.

**Realistic target.** Not Deep Made Simple's trajectory: its first upload took 43K and ours took
229, and that gap is distribution, not craft. The honest near-term goal is *repeatability* —
three consecutive uploads in one series, each beating the channel median of 62, and one beating
400. That is what "the format works" would look like at this size. Everything past that needs
CTR and retention data we currently cannot read.

inflated by post-fame back-catalogue traffic, and cut the comparison table that had used them as

**Pass 2, our own numbers.** Recomputed all medians from the raw listing rather than reusing the
August diagnosis: lifetime views 4,805 not 2,561, channel median 62, long-form median 127
against short-form 62, enumeration median 62. Found the 2,400-view video is 50% of the channel,
and that the description promises four surah series with no matching uploads.

**Pass 3, alignment.** Checked every recommendation against the standing rules: long-form only
per the owner's 2026-08-17 direction; winners protected per `packaging-fix.json`; 16:9 thumbnails
mandatory above 180 seconds; nothing ported from Finance % Decoded or HELD BY FAITH. Corrected
the runtime recommendation from the 27:40 figure in `video-formula.md` to the 17–22 band, and
noted why both numbers are right about different things. Downgraded the long-form and
format-shape findings from claims to tests, because one hit and one counter-example do not carry
a causal claim.
