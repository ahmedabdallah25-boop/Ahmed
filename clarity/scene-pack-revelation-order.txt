READ THE FOUR HARD RULES BEFORE YOU GENERATE ANYTHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. NO FACES. NO PROPHETS. NO COMPANIONS. Not stylised, not distant, not in
   silhouette-with-features, not "just the outline". Nothing in this pack depicts
   the Prophet ﷺ, any prophet, or any named Companion, and no prompt below asks
   for a human face of any kind. Where a person is unavoidable the pack uses
   hands, feet, a shadow on a wall, a figure at a distance so small it is a mark,
   or the space a person has just left. This is not a stylistic preference and it
   is not negotiable — on this subject it is the difference between a video that
   circulates and one that gets torn apart in its own comments.

2. NO PHOTOREAL ANYTHING. Ink and watercolour throughout. thumbnail-system.md §3
   already established why: this channel carries a public "AI BE AWARE" comment,
   and photoreal AI is the signature that comment is pointing at. A painting
   reads as somebody made this.

3. NO GENERATED TEXT INSIDE A GENERATED IMAGE. Image models produce nonsense
   Arabic and misspelt English, and on a Quran channel a garbled ayah on screen
   is unrecoverable. Every glyph the viewer sees — Arabic, English, numerals,
   labels — is TYPESET IN THE EDIT over a plate that was generated empty. Every
   prompt below therefore ends with the negative block, which forbids text.

4. THE ARABIC IS SET FROM A VERIFIED MUSHAF. Copy-paste from a checked digital
   text, then have a reader confirm it on screen before render. Never retype it.

THE FIXED STYLE BLOCK — append to EVERY illustration prompt, unchanged
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  STYLE: ink and watercolour on aged parchment. Loose confident pen line, wet
  bleeding washes with visible grain and hard edges where the water dried.
  Palette: raw sienna, burnt umber, ochre, warm grey, with sparing indigo and a
  single warm gold accent. Muted, aged, hand-made. Generous empty parchment.
  Cinematic 16:9, 1920x1080.

  NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no writing, no numerals, no calligraphy, no
  signature, no watermark, no human face, no facial features, no portrait, no
  photoreal skin, no 3D render, no digital gloss, no lens flare, no modern
  objects, no crosses, no church architecture, no menorah, no Star of David.

  (The last four exist because this channel's assets were transposed from a
  Christian channel once already and the artefacts leaked into public copy. The
  same leak in an image is worse, because nobody proof-reads a picture.)

HOW 135 PROMPTS BECOME ~400 VISUAL EVENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

video-formula.md measured the reference channel changing frame every 3–5 seconds.
Over 2,598 seconds that is 433–866 events. Generating 430 images would cost a
fortune and drift in style across the video, which is the exact failure the fixed
style block exists to prevent.

So each ILLUS scene yields FOUR events from ONE generation:

  1. LINE     — same prompt + "line art only, no colour, no wash", same seed
  2. WASH     — the prompt exactly as written
  3. PUSH     — the wash, slow 4% push-in
  4. DRIFT    — the wash, slow lateral drift across the grain

  Events 1→2 cross-dissolve over 1.5–2.5s: the ink appears, then the colour
  blooms into it. You are showing the drawing being made. It is native to this
  medium rather than imposed on it, and it is a signature the channel can own.
  Hold LINE while the VO sets the idea up; let the WASH bloom on the word that
  lands.

     90 ILLUS × 4 events           = 360
     38 GRAPHIC frames (in-edit)   =  38
      7 RECITATION frames          =   7
     ------------------------------------
     405 events over 2,598s        ≈ 6.4s average

  Tightest cutting in ACT 0 and ACT 7. Longest holds in ACT 3's narrative stretch.

GRAPHIC frames are NOT generated. They are built in the edit from typeset text on
the navy ground — same palette as the thumbnails: navy #16203C, gold #E8A33D,
cream #F2ECDC, rust #C0492B. Amiri for Arabic, Archivo for Latin. These are the
frames that carry the argument, and per video-formula.md §5 they are the reason
this video works at 43 minutes instead of collapsing into mood art.

RECITATION marks a place where the TTS stops and a real reciter is heard. Seven
of them, 86 seconds total. Never synthesise these.
