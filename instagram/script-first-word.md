## How it uses the seven devices

1. **It starts mid-conversation.** "By the way…" begins as if the talk had already started.
2. **The double-take is the hook.** "Death? You just woke up." The viewer hears the reaction before
   they have their own.
3. **One concrete picture.** The phone on the pillow.
4. **The turn.** What the eye sees is "I woke up". What Allah did is "He sent you back".
5. **One Arabic word, then its meaning.** *Amatana.*
6. **Short fragments and real pauses.** Hold after "woke up" and after "sent you back".
7. **It lands soft.** It ends on intimacy with Allah, not a call to action.

## Delivery

- Record it in one take and cut the picture to the voice.
- Use a close mic and a talking voice. "Death?" should sound genuinely surprised, not performed.
- Leave a full beat of silence before the quote and before the last line.
