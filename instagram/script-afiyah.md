# @brother_ahmed_abdullah: next Reel, "You've been making the wrong dua"

Drafted 2026-11-25. A talking-head Reel of about 35 seconds, written in Ousama Alshurafa's Shorts
pattern (taken from the transcripts of his 10 biggest Shorts).

## Script

| # | Voice | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You've been making the wrong dua. | **THE WRONG DUA** |
| 2 | Hear me out. | |
| 3 | We ask Allah for money. A spouse. A job. A house. | |
| 4 | Al-Abbas, the Prophet's ﷺ own uncle, asked him: "Teach me something to ask Allah for." He said: "Ask Allah for 'afiyah." | **"ASK FOR 'AFIYAH"** |
| 5 | Abbas came back days later and asked again. Same answer. | **SAME ANSWER** |
| 6 | Everything you ask for is a zero. 'Afiyah is the one in front of it. Without the one, a million zeros are worth nothing. | **1,000,000 → 0** |
| 7 | And 'afiyah isn't only health. It's being kept safe in your body, your deen, your family, your money, from things you never even saw coming. | |
| 8 | So keep asking for the job. Just put the one in front first. | |
| 9 | Tonight, before anything else: *Allahumma inni as'aluka al-'afiyah.* Send this to the person you'd make that dua for. | اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَافِيَةَ |

**Optional line after beat 2, if it's true for you:** "I learned this in a hospital bed." Use it
only if it is your own story.

**Source:** Tirmidhi 3514. Al-Abbas asks twice and gets the same answer the second time: "ask
Allah for well-being in this world and the Hereafter." Al-Albani graded it sahih. Abu Bakr's
version, "no one has been given anything better than 'afiyah after certainty," is in Ibn Majah
3849 and Tirmidhi 3558.

## How it follows his pattern

| His move | Example from his Shorts | In this script |
|---|---|---|
| A first line that sounds wrong | "Your boss doesn't pay you." | "You've been making the wrong dua." |
| "Hear me out." | Happiness Is Overrated | Beat 2 |
| One everyday image carries the idea | Job = mailbox | Zeros, with 'afiyah as the 1 |
| A small detail most people read past | Materialism versus the Unseen | Abbas asked twice and got the same answer |
| "Take the means, but…" | "Go to work… but" | "Keep asking for the job. Just…" |
| One instruction to finish | "Join me live" | Make the dua tonight, then send the Reel |

The ending asks people to *send* the Reel, not follow you, because sends to a friend are the
signal Instagram weighs most for reaching people who don't follow you yet. A dua you would make
for someone else is a natural thing to send.
