Drafted 2026-11-25, third draft: cut to 30 seconds maximum, about 75 words at his pace. Read it
out loud once before filming and change any word you wouldn't actually say.

I caught myself doing something every morning. And I'm not proud of it.

I open my eyes, and my hand's already on the phone. WhatsApp. Emails. Did the video do numbers.

Allah *just* gave me my life back. Sleep is the small death. And the first thing I did with
it... was check my notifications.

Whatever your hand reaches for first, when nobody's watching? That's what your heart's
attached to.

So. Seven days. Phone across the room. First word is Alhamdulillah.

Comment "day one" if you're in.

## What changed from draft one, and why

Draft one copied the structure of his scripted webinar-promo clips and came out sounding like a
template. His real voice, in his longer clips and in his Gaza piece (`Z7Bh45rCtcs`), works like
this:

- **He opens with a confession**, not a slogan. "I learned something about myself on October 7th…
  my heart said 'Mama' before it said 'Allah'."
- **One theme under everything:** where the heart actually turns, to creation or to the Creator.
- **He talks like a person.** "That's pretty intense." "He loves you, man." "Become a fanatic.
  Obsessed, OCD, praying on time."
- **He puts the line people will remember in the middle**, not at the end. "Emotions are powerful
  alarms, but horrible leaders."
- **He ends on a practical challenge.** "Start with your prayer… See how that changes your life."
