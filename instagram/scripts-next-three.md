# @brother_ahmed_abdullah: three more Reels in the spoken flow

Drafted 2026-11-25. These use the same format and rules as `script-first-word.md` and
`scripts-spoken-flow.pdf`. Each is 70–80 words, about **27–30 seconds** with the pauses. Read
each one aloud and swap any word you wouldn't say to a friend.

None of these sources repeats one already used in the PDF's 20 scripts.

---

## 1. "Kadhalika"

Qur'an 21:87–88 · Tirmidhi 3505

| Time | VO | Emphasis | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "You know what Yunus said inside the whale? He didn't say *get me out.*" | **get me out** | Black water, no light |
| 3–6s | "Dark night. Dark sea. Dark stomach. And he says:" | **dark** | Three slow fades to darker |
| 6–12s | "*There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.*" | — | One candle-sized light, held |
| 12–15s | "That's it. No list. Just... *it was me.*" | **me** | Hold |
| 15–19s | "Then Allah adds one line: *And thus do We save the believers.*" | — | Light spreads slowly |
| 19–23s | "*Kadhalika.* Thus. Like that. Not just him." | **kadhalika** | The Arabic word alone |
| 23–28s | "Whatever your whale is tonight... same three lines." | **tonight** | Back to black water → loop |

**Caption:** The dua in Arabic, لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ, with 21:87–88. Add
Tirmidhi 3505: "no Muslim ever supplicates with it for anything, except that Allah responds to him."

⚠ SCHOLAR CHECK: "No list" means he asked for nothing outright. Scholars still count it as a dua,
and Tirmidhi 3505 calls it one. The line describes its wording only. Confirm it reads that way.

## 2. "No shopping list"

Qur'an 28:23–27

| Time | VO | Emphasis | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "Musa made a dua with no shopping list." | **no list** | Empty desert, heat haze |
| 3–6s | "On the run. No money. No home. Didn't know a soul." | **nothing** | Footprints in sand |
| 6–9s | "Helps two women water their sheep. Then goes and sits... in the shade." | **shade** | A tree, one patch of shade |
| 9–15s | "*My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in need.*" | — | Hands open on knees, no face |
| 15–18s | "*Faqir.* In need. He didn't even say *what.*" | **faqir** | The Arabic word alone |
| 18–23s | "Few ayat later? A home. A job. A wife. [Same afternoon he walked in with nothing.]" | **a home** | Door opening onto a lit room |
| 23–28s | "Sometimes the whole dua is just... *I'm empty.*" | **empty** | Back to the shade → loop |

**Caption:** The ayah in Arabic, رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ, with 28:24.

⚠ NEW: "A home. A job. A wife." compresses 28:25–27 into one line. The marriage is offered in
28:27, but the timeline is a summary. The [bracketed] line overstates the speed, so cut it unless
a scholar is comfortable with it.

## 3. "As you think"

Bukhari 7405

| Time | VO | Emphasis | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "There's a hadith that honestly... scares me a little." | **scares** | Someone sitting on stairs, back to camera |
| 3–7s | "Allah says: *I am just as My slave thinks I am.*" | — | Text on dark |
| 7–10s | "As you *think?* Like... whatever I'm expecting?" | **think** | Hold |
| 10–13s | "*Dhann.* What you assume He's going to do." | **dhann** | The Arabic word alone |
| 13–19s | "So when you say *Allah's never fixing this*... you're the one who wrote that ending." | **ending** | Pen stops mid-line |
| 19–23s | "Not Him. You." | **you** | Hold |
| 23–28s | "Think good of Him. Right now. Before the next thing goes wrong." | **right now** | Stand up off the stairs → loop |

**Caption:** "I am just as My slave thinks I am, and I am with him if he remembers Me." (Bukhari 7405)

⚠ NEW: "You're the one who wrote that ending" is the common teaching drawn from this hadith, put
in your own words. It must not suggest that our thoughts control Allah's decree. Get it approved.

⚠ SCHOLAR CHECK: Check the quote against sunnah.com/bukhari:7405. Its bracketed gloss is left out.

Post **Kadhalika** first. It has the strongest double-take and the most familiar story. Post
**No shopping list** next, and frame the two as "the duas prophets made at their lowest", then
**As you think** after them. **The first word** stands on its own, so post it whenever suits you.
