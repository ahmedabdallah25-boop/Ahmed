Money Decoded launch kit

Fourteen posts, seven days. Tap a button, paste into X.

How to run this

One post at a time — not a thread. These are fourteen independent ideas with no narrative spine, and each diagram stands alone. A thread spends all of them on one shot at distribution; posted separately you get fourteen. Reply posts also travel far less than the head post, so 2–14 would be buried.

Two a day, roughly 9am and 7pm. Post 1 first, then pin it.

Alt text is not optional — a screen reader cannot read a chart. In the X app, tap the attached image, then +ALT.

Stay in your replies for 30 minutes after each post. Then go reply thoughtfully to twenty other people. That is the part that actually grows the account.

No links in the post body. If you want to point at the YouTube video, put it in a reply an hour later.
