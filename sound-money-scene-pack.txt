CAPTION BURN-IN SPEC
================================================================================

LINE 1 (top)    : white #FFFFFF, Montserrat or Inter Bold 700, sentence case,
                  on a solid #000000 box, tight padding, box sized to the text
                  (not full width)
LINE 2 (below)  : soft gold #F5D76E, ITALIC, own solid #000000 box, offset
                  approximately 20px to the left of line 1
POSITION        : lower third, above the YouTube UI band — keep clear of the
                  bottom 250px of a 1920px tall frame
BEHAVIOUR       : STATIC per phrase. No karaoke. No word-by-word pop. No colour
                  highlighting. No scaling or bouncing.
EMPHASIS        : italics only
TIMING          : swap on the speaker's natural clause break — the caption change
                  and the picture cut land together

Enable a multi-language audio track on upload. That is what produces YouTube's
"Auto-dubbed" chip, and it is free additional reach.


================================================================================
EDIT & ASSEMBLY
================================================================================

1. Record the VO first. The cut follows speech cadence, so scene timings will
   shift to match the actual delivery. Treat the timecodes above as a guide.
2. Generate scene 09, approve AMIR, lock the seed, then generate everything else.
3. HARD CUTS ONLY. No dissolves, no whip pans, no zoom transitions, no glitch.
4. Slow push-in or lateral drift on every scene. Nothing fully static.
5. Film grain overlay across the master.
6. Burn in captions per the spec above.
7. Open with a one-second callback beat to Part 14 if the hook allows — but the
   short must still work standalone for a viewer who never saw Part 14.
8. Master to media/sound-money-edited.mp4, captions to media/sound-money.srt.

  Scenes 01-04  the hook. Must create the mystery inside 3 seconds. If this
                doesn't land, nothing else matters.
  Scene 15      the mechanism payoff. The one frame that has to be understood.
  Scene 23      emotional peak and best thumbnail candidate.
