STRUCTURE — read this before anything else
================================================================================

  0:00-0:06  HOOK      Two lines. A cost the viewer is already paying.
  0:06-0:26  RECAP     "If you're new here..." — episode 14, then the pivot.
  0:26-0:42  CONTRAST  Two kinds of money: created vs earned.
  0:42-0:57  THE SWAP  How the weight was quietly removed from the paper.

  1:31-1:42  THE TRAP  The one move that undoes all of it, then the close.

The recap is not a summary — it is a second hook. It opens by naming the person
it is for — "If you're new here" — which does three things at once: it tells a new
viewer this is catch-up and not the video proper, it tells a returning viewer they
may lean back for fifteen seconds, and it quietly says there is a series behind
this. Then every line is "we said" / "we called it" / "we promised", so the recap
reads as this channel keeping its word rather than as filler.

A viewer who never saw episode 14 must get the whole mechanism from it, and a
viewer who did see it must feel recognition, not repetition. That is why these
scenes deliberately re-stage episode 14's own shots — the receipt, the letterbox.
Same images, faster cutting, and an ending episode 14 didn't have.

DELIVERY NOTES:
- Line 1 is the whole video's fate. Deliver it flat and certain, not dramatic.
  The pause before "and still end up poorer" is the hook, not the volume.
- Line 3 is warm and direct, spoken to one person — the "if you're new here" is an
  invitation, not an apology. Do not rush it; it is the line that earns the next
  fifteen seconds.
- Lines 4-7 run 15% faster than the rest of the video. Tighten the gaps in the
  edit until the recap feels like a countdown.
- Line 8 is the hinge of the video. Real silence before it, then "And we promised
  you the fix" warm and level, then a beat, then "This is it" softer and slower.
