ELEVENLABS v3 DELIVERY NOTES
- Every line carries a tag. One tag per line, at the front. NEVER stack two tags
  on one line — v3 blends them into mush or reads the second one aloud.
- Model: Eleven v3. Stability: CREATIVE. On Robust the tags are largely ignored;
  on Creative they actually move the performance, which is the entire point here.
- TEST SCENES 01-03 BEFORE GENERATING THE REST. If any tag is spoken out loud
  instead of performed, the voice or the stability setting is wrong — fix it
  there, not by deleting tags from all 38 lines.
- Ellipses are breath pauses and they carry the timing of this script. Keep them
  exactly where they are. CAPS (scene 25, "That IS the product") carry emphasis
  without needing a tag.
- The emotional arc the tags are drawing, in order:
    curiosity → suspicion → indictment → grief → calm → resolution
  Scenes 01-09 pull the viewer in. 10-21 tighten and accuse. 22-27 drop into
  sadness — that is the retention floor and it must feel heavy. 28-38 lift back
  into warmth so the payoff lands as relief rather than a lecture.
- FALLBACKS if a tag misfires on your voice: [deadpan] → [calm] · [rushed] →
  [nervously] · [dramatic] → [emphatic] · [nervously] → [whispers].
- Do not tag the two silent beats. Let the picture hold.
