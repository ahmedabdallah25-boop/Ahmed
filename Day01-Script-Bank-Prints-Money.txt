--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  8. PRODUCTION NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- HOOK IS EVERYTHING: the on-screen text must hit in frame 1, before the VO.
- STANDALONE: zero references to "last video / part one." This works cold.
- CAPTIONS: burn word-by-word subtitles (most Shorts are watched muted).
- MUSIC: low, tense, sub-bass drone; duck it -6dB under the voice.
- CUT ON EVERY LINE: new image each beat above = ~8 cuts = high retention.
- LOOP: end frame (the eye) can hard-cut back to the Scene 1 pen — seamless
  rewatch = more watch time = more reach.
- LENGTH: keep it UNDER 60s so it stays eligible as a Short.
