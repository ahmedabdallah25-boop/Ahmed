# Pension script — viral audit and retag

**Audited 2026-08-09.** Subject: the VO script in
[`pension-scene-pack.txt`](pension-scene-pack.txt), *"Is Your Pension Halal? (You Never Picked
What It Buys)"*, target publish 2026-08-09 03:00 PT.

**Scope of the change made:** emotion tags only. **Not one word of the script was altered,
added or removed.** Every recommendation that would touch copy is listed in §7 and left
unapplied.

---

## 1 · The verdict, stated honestly first

**Viral probability is low, and the script is not the binding constraint.**

Defining "viral" for this channel as ≥1,000 views — roughly 5x the sub count and the level
`riQ7dLSnogo` (1,225) and the two 1.2K winners reached — I put this upload at **roughly 3–7%**.
The most likely 72-hour outcome is **30–80 views**.

That is not a judgement on the writing. It is what the channel's own ledger says. From
`channel-reset.md` fifth pass: five consecutive new-subject/new-format uploads landed in a
**6–22 views/day band regardless of family, claim, or title shape**. The `Is [X] Halal?` format
won its designed head-to-head **2.2x** (13.9 v/day vs 6.2) — a real result, and still inside
the same losing band. A script cannot out-write a distribution problem.

So read this audit as *margin work*. Pre-publish packaging is, per the fifth pass, the only
lever with a measured non-zero return, and it is the one that was bypassed on all four of the
Aug 5–7 uploads. It is worth doing well. It is not worth expecting a different band from.

**What would actually move the number** is unchanged and already written down: one month of
paid analytics to separate topic from cadence from retention. Four passes of inference from
public view counts could not, and two of those passes had to retract a conclusion.

### What this script has going for it

- **The glide-path reveal is a genuinely rare claim.** "The fund is built to move you *deeper*
  into interest as you get older, automatically, as a feature" — almost no competitor video in
  this niche makes this point at all. It is the same mechanism-reveal shape that produced IFG's
  single best video (221K).
- **It ends on a two-minute checklist.** Nothing in the 65-channel competitor map does. That is
  the save-and-share engine, and it is the strongest structural asset in the pack.
- **It bookends.** Line 4 "You were opted in" returns as line 44 "You were opted in by default."
  Most Shorts have no structure at all.
- **The subject is universal and unserved.** ~3,220 total results for the whole query; the top
  performer is nine years old.

## 2 · The hook — first 14 seconds, line by line

```
[0:00-0:05]  Your pension owns things you would never buy.
[0:05-0:08]  And nobody asked you.
[0:08-0:10]  Not once.
[0:10-0:14]  You were opted in. Including into this.
```

**Line one is strong.** Possessive reversal ("your pension owns"), concrete, second person,
and it implies something concealed without promising a number it has to pay off. It survives
being read cold. Keep it.

Four defects follow, in descending order of cost.

### 2.1 Frame one is the weakest frame in the pack — and frame one is the whole gate

`next-slate.md` sets the doctrine: Shorts have no thumbnail, so **frame one and line one are
the gate**, and the swipe happens inside ~3 seconds. Scene 01 is a top-down blank cream
document with a blank rounded rectangle and five blank cards. No character, no face, no
saturated colour, no motion beyond a slow push, and the pack's own prompt specifies "no text
anywhere." It is the lowest-information frame in the file, held for five seconds, at the exact
moment the decision is made.

Meanwhile **scene 04's image** — a large close-up of a checkbox already filled with a bold rust
red machine-printed tick, "the only saturated colour in the frame" — is the single frame in the
pack that says *someone already decided this for you* with no words at all. It is sitting at
0:10.

**Recommended (no copy change): open on scene 04's image under line one.** Scene 01's blank
document then works better where scene 04 currently is, as the thing the tick was printed on.
This is a scene-order change, not a script change, and it is the highest-value single edit
available on this video.

### 2.2 The hook runs at 90 words per minute

21 words across 14 seconds. Line one alone is 7 words in 5 seconds. Platform hooks run
150–180 wpm; even documentary narration runs 130–150. At 90 wpm the opening does not read as
gravitas, it reads as dead air, and dead air in seconds 0–3 is indistinguishable from a video
that has not started.

**Recommended: run the hook at ~135 wpm (≈9.5s for the same four lines) and give the recovered
4.5 seconds to the glide-path reveal**, where the pack itself says to slow down. No words cut.

### 2.3 `[whispers]` at 0:05 was an audibility risk — **fixed**

Shorts play through phone speakers, frequently in public, frequently at low volume. ElevenLabs
v3's whisper drops well below the rest of the mix. If line 2 is inaudible, lines 1 → 3 do not
connect and the hook has no second beat. Changed to `[quietly]`: same intimacy, no dropout.
Nudge that line +3 dB in the mix regardless.

### 2.4 "Including into this" is the hook's weak link — **flagged, not changed**

"This" has no referent yet. Nothing has been shown, named or described. It lands as a
grammatical stumble at second 10, and stumbles in a hook cost more than anywhere else in a
video. The clean versions are *"Including into this basket"* (which also plants the word the
whole middle section runs on) or simply stopping at *"You were opted in."*

Not changed, per instruction. Worth one minute of the user's time before the VO is recorded,
because it is cheap now and impossible later.

### 2.5 The structural point about the noun

The slate's finding is that **the specificity of the noun is the whole game** — `Is Forex
Halal?` did 158K where `What do we really mean by Halal Investing?` did 6.4K. "Pension" is
specific. But specific is not the same as *urgent*, and pension is the one personal-finance
noun that is deferred by construction — a Shorts audience skewing under 35 has no felt cost
today.

The script does fix this, twice, and both fixes are late: line 5 universalises it
("Four-oh-one-k. Workplace plan.") at 0:14, and line 34 makes it present-tense and active
("The machine moves you deeper into it as you get older") at 2:10. **The material for a
stronger hook is already inside this script at position 34.** Nothing needs writing.

## 3 · Composition

The arc is sound and it is better built than most Shorts in this niche:

| Beat | Time | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:14 | The claim, and the accusation |
| Universalise | 0:14–0:30 | Pension / 401(k) / workplace plan, same machine |
| The default fund | 0:30–0:51 | You chose the number, not the destination |
| **First payoff** | 0:51–0:56 | *"Nobody screens it. That is the entire point of it."* |
| What's in the basket | 0:56–1:10 | Banks, insurers, alcohol, gambling, tobacco, weapons |
| The sharp part | 1:10–1:35 | A bank's product is interest |
| **The reveal** | 1:35–2:16 | Lifestyling / glide path — deeper into interest by design |
| **The turn** | 2:21–2:25 | *"So. Two minutes. Three steps."* |
| Three steps | 2:25–3:00 | Log in → holdings → sharia option |
| Close | 3:00–3:12 | Bookend, then the CTA |

Four observations.

**3.1 The strongest asset sits at 66% depth.** The glide path lands at 2:10 of a 3:16 video.
In the niche's 221K winner the mechanism reveal *is* the hook. The retention checkpoint list in
the pack acknowledges scene 34 is the best thing in the file and then buries it behind two
minutes of setup. **Recommended (no copy change): a 1.5-second silent cold-open flash of scene
34's image — Bilal walking the glide path toward the faceless clerk — before scene 01, with no
VO over it.** Front-loads the best frame, costs nothing, spoils nothing.

**3.2 The title's promise is paid at 2:44 of 3:16.** The words "sharia or Islamic" appear
exactly once, in line 41, at 84% depth. A viewer who clicked *Is Your Pension Halal?* waits the
entire video for the word in the title. This is partly deliberate — `CLAUDE.md` frames the
channel as Islamic finance taught *through universal money problems*, and the riba argument
does arrive at 1:10. But the cost lands specifically on this video because this title makes an
explicit halal promise and the body defers it longer than any previous upload. Flagging, not
resolving: the fix is a copy change and copy is out of scope.

**3.3 There is no question asked of the viewer.** Zero comment-bait in 315 words. The only
question in the script (line 12, "So what is actually in it?") is rhetorical and self-answered
four seconds later. At 193 subscribers, comments are a distribution input, not a vanity metric.
This does not need a script change — **put the question in the pinned comment and the
description**: *"Go and look — what's the biggest holding in your default fund?"* That is
answerable in four words by someone who just did the two-minute check, which is exactly the
audience the ending creates.

**3.4 The three-step block is the reason to publish this.** It is specific, free, reversible,
and takes two minutes. "Two minutes" is stated three times. That repetition is correct and
should not be trimmed — it is the save trigger.

## 4 · Two production defects found in the pack itself

Both are numbers the pack asserts about itself that do not survive checking.

### 4.1 The video is storyboarded at 3:16, not ~165s — and 3:16 is outside the band

The header, and `next-slate.md` entry 2, both state **~165s, "inside the 133–180s band."** The
46 scene timecodes are continuous with no gaps or overlaps and total **196 seconds — 3:16**.
That is **16 seconds outside** the band being cited as the justification for the length.

This matters because the band is the pack's stated reason for not cutting. If the honest number
had been 196s, the length decision would have needed a different argument. Worth correcting
before anyone reasons from "165s" again.

### 4.2 The script is 315 words, not ~350

Tags stripped, counted: **315**. At the claimed 165s that is 115 wpm; against the storyboarded
192s of VO it is **98 wpm**. Both are well under platform pace.

There is a real tension here that the pack does not name. `channel-reset.md` concluded
**"Length is noise; cadence is the signal"** — the 133–180s band is a correlation observed
across five winners, *not* a target to pad toward. 315 words delivered at a normal 150 wpm is
126s. The video is being stretched by roughly 40–70 seconds of silence to reach a band its own
analysis calls noise.

**Recommended: deliver at 130–140 wpm, land near 145s, keep every word, and spend the recovered
time entirely on the glide-path reveal** rather than spreading it evenly across 46 scenes.
145s is inside the band honestly rather than by padding.

## 5 · One accuracy gap the pack's notes do not cover

The ACCURACY NOTES correctly weaken *"one of the biggest slices"* (not "the largest") and
correctly refuse a number on *"most people never move."* Both are good calls.

They do not cover **line 18: "Alcohol. Gambling. Tobacco. Weapons."**

The claim as written is that the basket *holds* these — which is true, and the script never
states a weight. But in a broad cap-weighted index these are collectively low single-digit
percent, and the line's placement immediately after *"Banks and insurers. One of the biggest
slices in it"* invites the listener to carry the weight across. The first hostile comment on
this video will be *"that's about 2% of the index."*

**Recommended: add a line to ACCURACY NOTES** — the claim is presence, not weight; do not let
the delivery imply otherwise; if challenged in comments, the honest answer is that screening is
about what you own at all, and the financials weight is the part that is large. No script
change needed. The `[slowly]` tag now on that line helps, because deliberate enumeration reads
as *listing what is in there* rather than *claiming it dominates*.

## 6 · What was changed: the tags

**13 tags → 23. Zero words touched.**

The old distribution was the problem: **8 of 13 tags were `[serious]`**. A tag repeated eight
times across 315 words is not direction, it is the baseline — so v3 had effectively one register
for the entire video. That flat-serious texture is the "AI narrator" sound the platform is
trained to swipe. Three of the four biggest lines in the script carried no tag at all.

### The register system

Four registers, deliberately reused so the delivery reads as a system rather than as scattered
colour. It maps onto the pack's own visual grammar from `house-style.md` — *named people have
faces and colour, the institution has neither* — by flattening the **voice** wherever it speaks
for the machine.

| Register | Tags | Used for | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Machine** | `[flat]` `[matter-of-fact]` | describing what the system does | 5 |
| **Confiding** | `[quietly]` | speaking to the viewer as a person | 3 |
| **Weight** | `[emphatic]` `[firm]` | the load-bearing claims only | 8 |
| **Accent** | `[curious]` `[slowly]` `[brighter]` `[calm]` `[warmly]` | one-off turns | 7 |

`[emphatic]` appears **exactly four times** — lines 9, 15, 31, 34 — and those are the four
claims the video is built on. A fifth would cost all four their weight.

### Density follows the retention checkpoints

Not evenly spread. **4 tags across the 4 hook lines**, **8 across the glide-path reveal
(scenes 25–36)**, and long untagged runs through the setup and the checklist. The bare lines
are what make the tagged ones audible — which is what the original TAG NOTES meant by "sparse,"
and what eight `[serious]`es prevented.

### The seven changes that carry most of the value

| # | Line | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 02 *"And nobody asked you."* | `[whispers]` | `[quietly]` | v3's whisper drops below the mix on phone speakers. Inaudible line 2 kills the hook. |
| 2 | 18 *"Alcohol. Gambling. Tobacco. Weapons."* | *(none)* | `[slowly]` | The most loaded line in the script had no direction. One word per beat is the moment. |
| 3 | 36 *"So. Two minutes. Three steps."* | `[calm]` | `[brighter]` | `[calm]` after a heavy reveal reads as deflation. This is the gear change that converts dread into a save. |
| 4 | 41 *"…a sharia or Islamic option."* | *(none)* | `[slowly]` | The only line that pays the title's promise, previously untagged. Checkpoint 4 says it "must be understood, not just heard." |
| 5 | 01 *"Your pension owns things…"* | `[serious]` | `[matter-of-fact]` | An outrageous claim stated plainly is credible; stated with gravel it is clickbait-voice. Understatement is the contrast engine for the whole script. |
| 6 | 22 *"It is interest."* | `[serious]` | `[quietly]` | Three words carrying the channel's entire thesis. Dropping the volume lands it harder than pushing it. |
| 7 | 25 *"…the part almost nobody knows."* | `[serious]` | `[quietly]` | The confiding drop is the standard move into a reveal, and it sets up `[emphatic]` six lines later. |

Full mapping, mechanics, and a per-tag fallback table are in the rewritten **TAG NOTES** block
in `pension-scene-pack.txt`, directly under the VO script.

### Before recording

`[matter-of-fact]`, `[flat]`, `[firm]` and `[brighter]` are descriptive rather than canonical v3
tags. v3 handles descriptive tags well but can occasionally **speak one aloud**. Test-render
**lines 1–4 and 29–36 only** before committing the full 315 words; the fallback swaps are in the
TAG NOTES. The one tag genuinely worth A/B-ing is line 1 — `[matter-of-fact]` against `[firm]`.

## 7 · Recommended, deliberately not applied

Everything here needs either a copy change or a decision that is the user's to make.

| # | Recommendation | Type | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open on scene 04's image (the pre-printed rust tick), move scene 01 to 0:10 | scene order | **highest** |
| 2 | 1.5s silent cold-open flash of scene 34 (glide path) before the hook | edit | high |
| 3 | Deliver at 130–140 wpm → ~145s; give the recovered time to scenes 25–36 | pacing | high |
| 4 | Fix *"Including into this"* → *"Including into this basket"*, or cut the second sentence | **copy** | high, cheap now |
| 5 | Put the comment question in the pinned comment and description, not the VO | metadata | medium |
| 6 | Correct the pack header and `next-slate.md`: 196s storyboarded, not ~165s | docs | medium |
| 7 | Add the presence-vs-weight note to ACCURACY NOTES for line 18 | docs | medium |
| 8 | +3 dB on line 2 in the mix | audio | low, free |

**Not recommended:** cutting the script to 60s. That rule was tested against this channel's own
numbers and found backwards — four of the five best videos run 133–180s. Do not cut words to
hit a runtime; slow the pace less instead.

## 8 · Reading the result

Publish per the pack. Then, per `channel-reset.md`:

- **Do not judge it before 72 hours.** Channel listing publish dates run a day off; a four-hour
  old upload can look dead.
- **Read marginal views/day between passes, not the lifetime average.** `kOkfpCHeURw` reported
  a healthy ~124/day lifetime while its marginal rate had already decayed 4x.
- **Two variables move on this upload, not one** — subject *and* render style, plus now the tag
  system. If it over- or under-performs, it cannot be attributed to the subject. Say so.
- **If it lands in the 6–22 v/day band like the last five**, the script was never the
  constraint, and the answer is the paid analytics month, not another rewrite.
