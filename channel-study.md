## What the five winners share

All five 900+ view videos follow the same pattern:

**1. Topic = the viewer's own money.** Savings accounts, mortgages, inflation, passive income —
universal, high-emotion subjects that apply to every viewer in the feed, Muslim or not. The
Islamic-finance mechanism is the *answer*, not the topic.

**2. The first line is a personal-stakes paradox.** Verbatim hooks:

- Part 10: "Your savings account is the safest place to slowly lose everything."
- Part 8: "Imagine there's a mortgage where your debt gets smaller while you sleep."
- Part 3: "There's a tax you pay every single year. You never voted for it."
- Part 9: "I get paid every month like a bank, but I've never lent a single dollar."
- Part 4: "In 2008, the banking system needed a $700 billion rescue just to stay alive. Gold has never needed one."

Second person ("your savings", "you pay") or aspirational first person, and an
impossible-sounding claim — all inside the first 3 seconds, before the swipe decision.

**3. Title formula: `How to [benefit] Without [pain]` or `How Banks [harm] Your [money]`.**
The title's first words are the viewer's outcome. The Arabic term is relegated to a
"(X Explained)" suffix — branding for the warm audience, not the hook for the cold one.

**4. Series recap comes *after* the hook,** compressed to ~5 seconds ("Quick recap if you're
new here…"), and endings close with a forward tease + the "No jargon, just mechanisms" signature.

## Why the insurance arc is underperforming

**Part 11 (237 views, ~118/day):**

- Hook: "You can't sell a fish that's still in the sea." A metaphor with zero personal stakes —
  the viewer doesn't know what it costs *them* until much later.
- The hook flows straight into series bookkeeping ("in part five… in part six…") — meaningless
  to the ~99% of Shorts-feed impressions who are cold viewers on a 172-subscriber channel.
- Topic: *gharar* (contract uncertainty) — a fiqh concept, not a felt money problem.

**Part 12 (5 views, hours old):**

- Hook: "I'm your insurance company. My favorite customer pays every month and never ever calls."
  The villain-POV device is fine, but the payoff ("everything you don't claim is mine") lands at
  ~9–13 seconds — past the swipe window. Winners put the sting in line 1.
- Title starts company-centric ("Insurance Keeps the Money…") instead of the winning
  "Your…/How to…" viewer-first framing.
- **Packaging regressions vs. every winner:** the description dropped the full 12-part series
  list (only 3 links, no playlist URL), and the ending CTA points *backward* ("watch Part 11")
  — sending the few viewers it gets to the channel's weakest video instead of teasing what's next.
- Insurance is also structurally a "grudge topic": people resent paying for it and avoid
  thinking about it. It can work, but only when framed as *money you're losing right now*.

**Important caveat:** 5 views on a Short published this morning is mostly recency — Shorts
routinely sit near zero for 1–3 days before the feed tests them. The video is not yet proven
dead; the packaging problems above just lower its odds when the test comes. See
`last-video-fix.md` for the action plan and the 72-hour decision rule.

## Housekeeping issues found

1. **Possibly broken playlist link everywhere.** The channel description and most video
   descriptions link `https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKLKzR1QgFHE`. Real playlist IDs
   are ~34 characters — this one looks truncated. If it 404s, every video on the channel points
   to a dead link. Verify and fix in: channel About, and each video description.
2. **Part numbering vs. upload order** (Parts 3–4 posted mid-series). Harmless for the feed, but
   the "series so far" lists in older descriptions should be kept consistent so binge navigation works.
3. **Momentum risk:** two consecutive below-average videos can cool Shorts distribution. The next
   upload matters more than usual — make it a proven-formula topic (see recommendations).

## Topic recommendations (next uploads)

Return to universal money mechanics with the halal fix as the reveal — the proven lane:

1. **Car financing** — "Your car loan charges you rent on money. Here's how to drive without it (Ijara)."
2. **Salary/inflation** — "Your raise was 5%. You still got poorer. Here's the mechanism."
3. **Student loans** — the debt trap framing, then Qard Hasan / income-share alternatives.
4. **Zakat vs. tax** — "One system taxes your income. The other taxes hoarded wealth. Only one has ever worked."
5. **Buy-now-pay-later** — "Klarna didn't invent this. It's a 1,400-year-old contract — done wrong."

Keep fiqh-heavy concept videos (gharar, contract theory) to at most every third upload, and
always package them through a felt money problem.
