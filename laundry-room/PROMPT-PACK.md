# Prompt pack — generate these yourself

Copy-paste prompts for your own image tool. Built from the one render that came out right, with
its faults corrected.

---

## Reference: the one render that worked

The 9 Aug portrait hero. Match it — corridor proportion, tank on a steel frame at the far end,
green fire exit over a grey door, rigid galvanised ducting, yellow walkway lines, grey resin
floor with coved skirting, tiled dado, cable tray and batten overhead. Its only faults: **4
dryers drawn instead of 3**, **no horizontal header** joining the duct drops, and **one batten
line instead of two rows**. Every prompt below corrects those.

---

## The rule that makes or breaks it

**Use a tall PORTRAIT aspect ratio — 3:4 or 2:3. Never 16:9.**

Every wide-format attempt turned the room into an open hall. Portrait forces the corridor
perspective. This matters more than any wording in the prompt.

**Second rule:** state the machine counts as numerals *and* words, and repeat them at the end.
Models miscount constantly — four of five attempts got the counts wrong.

## Block A — paste this into EVERY prompt

```
CRITICAL GEOMETRY: a long narrow corridor room, 9 metres long but only 4.3 metres wide,
about 4 metres high. Machines line both long walls and their fronts are only 2.1 metres
apart, so the machines crowd both the left and right edges of the frame and the floor is a
narrow strip between them. This is a TIGHT CORRIDOR, not a wide hall. There is NO open
floor anywhere except the narrow central walkway. There is exactly ONE doorway in the
entire room.

MATERIALS: poured grey epoxy resin floor with coved skirting; pale grey tiled dado to 1.3 m;
brilliant white washable paint above; ceiling services, cable trays and conduit all deep
cleaned and painted one uniform matt light grey; linear LED batten fittings in TWO rows
running the length, 4000K neutral white, bright and even.

STYLE: realistic architectural interior photography, true-to-life materials, restrained and
functional, NOT a luxury showroom. Immaculate, no lint, nothing stored on the floor.
No people, no plants, no marble, no wood, no wire trolleys, no second doorway.
```

## 1. Hero — from the blind end toward the door ⭐ **portrait 3:4**

> Photorealistic interior photograph of a narrow commercial hotel laundry, shot from a HIGH
> VIEWPOINT near the ceiling at the blind end of the room, angled DOWNWARD along the length
> toward the far end.
> ON THE LEFT WALL: exactly 4 (four) light-grey Electrolux washer-extractors with brushed
> stainless steel tops and blue control panels.
> ON THE RIGHT WALL: exactly 3 (three) large light-grey Electrolux tumble dryers with big
> round doors. Above them a straight RIGID galvanised HORIZONTAL HEADER DUCT runs along the
> wall near the ceiling, with three short rigid vertical drops connecting up into it — the
> drops must join a horizontal header, not just rise into the ceiling.
> AT THE FAR END: a white cylindrical bulk tank on a painted steel support frame mounted high,
> a green running-man FIRE EXIT sign, and a single grey door below.
> Crisp painted yellow walkway lines running the length of the floor.
> Count check: 4 washers on the left, 3 dryers on the right. No more, no fewer.
> [+ Block A]

*This is the money shot — it pairs directly with your own top-view photo as a before/after.*

## 2. Reverse — from the doorway looking in **portrait 3:4**

> ON THE RIGHT: exactly 4 (four) light-grey Electrolux washer-extractors with stainless tops.

## 5. Dryer line detail **landscape 3:2 — the one exception**

Close-ups may use landscape; there is no corridor to get wrong.

> Photorealistic detail of exactly 3 (three) large light-grey Electrolux tumble dryers side by
> side against a wall, each with a big round door and a blue control panel, identification
> plates reading D1, D2 and D3. Above them a straight RIGID galvanised rectangular header duct

> with a small printed sign reading LINT TRAP - CLEAN EVERY CYCLE. Aluminium coved trim seals
> the machine bases to the resin floor.
> Count check: 3 dryers, 3 lint bins.
> [+ Block A materials and style lines only]

## Faults to watch for

Check every output against this list before accepting it.

| Fault | Seen in |
|---|---|
| **Room becomes a wide open hall** | Every 16:9 attempt. Switch to portrait. |
| **Wrong machine count** | 4 of 5 attempts. Five or six washers instead of four; four dryers instead of three. |
| **Ducts rise into the ceiling with no horizontal header** | The otherwise-correct hero. |
| **A second doorway appears** | Clean staging, twice. |
| **Lint bin fitted between washers** | W1/W2 detail. Washers make no lint. |
| **Wire trolleys** | Several. You own none. |
| **Shadow board labels duplicated** | Service station — BROOM and LINT BRUSH each twice. |
| **Garbled sign text** | Checklist card read "Flevor Removed". Treat all rendered text as layout only and retype for print. |
