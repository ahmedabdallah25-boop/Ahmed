# Laundry room → 5-star standard, minimum spend

Back-of-house commercial laundry. Electrolux washer-extractors and tumble dryers on plinths
down one side, linen trolley/cage storage down the other, exposed services overhead.

> Costs below are UAE market estimates in AED for a room of roughly this size (est. 12–15 m
> run, 4–6 machines). Get three quotes before committing to anything in Tier 3. Anything
> touching electrical supply, gas, or the extract system must go to a licensed contractor —
> price it, don't DIY it.

---

## 1. What the photos actually show

Ranked by how much each one costs you in perceived standard:

| # | Observation | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lint and fluff piled along the black machine plinths and floor edges** | The single loudest "not 5-star" signal in the room, and a genuine fire load. Visible in all three photos. |
| 2 | **Flexible corrugated aluminium ducting**, sagging, taped joints, running at both low and high level | Corrugations trap lint. This is the #1 cause of commercial laundry fires. Also reads as "temporary" forever. |
| 3 | **Ceiling is visually chaotic** — black plenum, silver flex hose, green pipe, bare conduit, all different finishes | Your eye reads clutter before it reads anything else. |
| 4 | **Bare fluorescent battens**, uneven spacing, warm/yellow cast, far end noticeably dark | Bad light makes clean surfaces look dirty. Cheapest high-impact fix in the room. |
| 5 | **Ad-hoc signage** — A4 paper taped up, mismatched hazard stickers at random heights | Inconsistency is what auditors and GMs notice. |
| 6 | **Loose documents/manuals lying on the floor** | Reads as abandoned. Free to fix. |
| 7 | **Mismatched wire cages/trolleys** stored leaning on the wall, some liners torn | No parking discipline = no zone discipline. |
| 8 | **Walls patchy and scuffed**, no washable finish, no splashback behind machines | Can't be cleaned properly, so it never looks clean. |
| 9 | **Grout dirty**, floor unzoned, no marked walkway or clean/soiled segregation | Segregation is a hotel-standard requirement, not a nice-to-have. |
| 10 | **Machine tops used as storage**, items on floor | Every flat surface becomes a shelf unless you give people a real one. |

**One counter-intuitive detail:** the plinths are dark/black, which makes white lint maximally
visible. Repainting them **mid-grey** makes the room look cleaner between cleans, for the price
of a tin of paint.

## 2. The principle to spend against

A five-star back-of-house room is not a luxurious room. It is a **consistent** one.
Same light, same height, same finish, same label, nothing loose. Luxury materials in a
laundry read as waste; consistency reads as control.

So the money goes to: **light, uniformity, labelling, and lint removal.** Not to finishes.

Roughly **60% of the visual gain here costs nothing at all** — it's Tier 0.

---

## 3. Tier 0 — AED 0. Do this first, this week.

Do not spend a dirham until this is done, because it changes what you actually need to buy.

- **Strip the lint. Everywhere.** Plinth tops, plinth bases, behind and under machines, wall
  ledges, duct exteriors, the backs of the trolleys. Then put it on a written schedule:
  lint traps every cycle, floor edges every shift, behind-machine sweep weekly.
- **Clear every horizontal surface.** Machine tops, plinth ledges, floor. Nothing lives on the
  floor except something with wheels.
- **Get the manuals off the floor.** One folder, one hook, one wall.
- **Park the trolleys in a line**, all facing the same way, against one wall only.
- **Remove every piece of paper that is not current**, and straighten what remains — even
  untidy signage looks 70% better simply aligned to a common top edge.
- **Deep clean the grout** with an alkaline tile cleaner and a stiff brush.
- **Set the segregation rule now** — soiled enters at one end, clean leaves at the other, and
  they never cross. Do it with discipline first; you'll mark it with tape in Tier 1.

Photograph the room after this. You will be surprised how much of the problem was free.

**Effort:** two staff, one shift. **Cost:** AED 0.

## 5. Tier 2 — about AED 3,000–5,000 more. Fixing the ceiling and the walls.

- **Replace flexible dryer ducting with rigid galvanised spiral duct**, smooth bore, minimum
  bends, properly supported on threaded rod at even spacing, with accessible cleaning ports.
  ~AED 80–130 per 3m section plus clamps and hangers; **budget AED 1,500–2,500 installed.**
  Treat this as a safety item, not a cosmetic one — it is the highest-value line in the whole
  document.
- **Paint all exposed ceiling services one uniform matt light grey.** Duct, conduit, brackets,
  pipe, hangers — all of it, one colour. This is the single biggest visual change per dirham
  in the room. **AED 400 in paint**, one weekend of labour.
- **Hygienic wall cladding behind the machine line**, 1.2m high. PVC hygienic sheet or
  stainless-look ACP is a fraction of real stainless and wipes down the same.
  2440×1220 sheets at AED 120–200. **Budget AED 900–1,400 including trim.**
- **Aluminium angle trim / kick plate along the plinth base**, sealed to the floor with a coved
  silicone edge so lint has nowhere to lodge. ~AED 30/m. **Budget AED 500.**

## 6. Tier 3 — about AED 4,000–7,000 more. The finishing layer.

- **Shadow board** for brushes, squeegee, dustpan, lint brush — white PVC foam board with
  printed vinyl silhouettes and hooks. **AED 350.**
- **PPE station** — wall-mounted acrylic bins, gloves/aprons/eye protection, signed. **AED 400.**
- **Chemical shelf with spill-containment bund tray**, uniform decanted-free containers with
  matching labels, plus **eyewash station**. **AED 900–1,500.**
- **Standardise the trolleys** — you don't need new cages. Repaint or replace *liners* only so
  they match, and retire anything torn. **AED 100–150 per liner.**
- **Framed noticeboard** with a laminated daily/weekly check grid, replacing all taped paper.
  **AED 400.**
- **Ventilation and heat** — a laundry in the UAE gets brutal. Confirm make-up air is adequate
  and add a wall-mounted drum fan if the far end stagnates. **AED 300–600**, but if the room
  runs hot, get the extract properly assessed — that's a contractor job.
- **Handwash basin** with soap and paper towel at the clean-linen end, if there isn't one.
  Usually a hotel-standard requirement. **AED 800–1,500 plumbed.**

## 9. Don't spend money on

- **New machines.** Yours are fine and they photograph fine once clean.
- **Real stainless splashback.** Hygienic PVC or stainless-look ACP is a fifth of the price and
  wipes down identically.
- **Retiling the floor.** The tile is serviceable — it's the grout and the absence of zoning
  that reads badly.
- **New trolleys.** Match the liners, not the frames.
- **Decorative anything.** Plants, feature walls, colour accents. Back-of-house five-star is
  clinical, not decorated. Restraint *is* the aesthetic.
- **Ceiling tiles / suspended ceiling.** Painting the exposed services grey gets you 80% of the
  effect for 5% of the cost, and keeps everything accessible for maintenance.

---

## 10. Safety items that aren't optional

Separate these from the aesthetics budget — they get done regardless.

- Rigid, cleanable dryer ducting with access ports. Lint in flexible duct is the classic
  commercial laundry fire.
- Lint traps cleaned every cycle, on a signed check sheet.
- CO₂ extinguisher near the electrical panels, with marked keep-clear floor hatching.
- Nothing stored against or on top of the machines, and clear access to every isolator and
  emergency stop.
- Clean/soiled segregation maintained physically, not just by intention.
- Eyewash within reach of the chemical store.

Verify the full list against **Dubai Municipality / Civil Defence requirements and your brand
standard** — the above is good practice, not a compliance certificate.

## 11. Four-week sequence

| Week | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Tier 0 in full. Photograph before and after. Order Tier 1 items. |
| 2 | Lights, plinth paint, wall paint, grout. Do it over a low-occupancy period. |
| 3 | Floor marking and zoning — after painting, never before. Install signage at one common height. |
| 4 | Ducting contractor. Ceiling services painted grey. Cladding if budget allows. |
| Ongoing | Daily check sheet on the noticeboard, signed. Without this, the room reverts in six weeks. |

The last row is the one that actually determines whether this holds. Everything above is a
one-off; the check sheet is what makes it permanent.
