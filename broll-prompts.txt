================================================================================
HOW TO USE THEM IN THE EDIT
================================================================================
- Hold each still 2.5-3.5s with a slow 4-6% push in, or a 40px drift. Longer
  reads as a stall.
- Under on-screen type: image at 35-45% opacity over the dark bed, or a #06080B
  scrim at 55% on top of it. Full-frame only in the gaps between text beats.
- Do NOT cut away between 2:18 and 3:10 — the amortisation chart is that whole
  scene and needs to play uninterrupted.
- Save as video/public/broll/01.jpg ... 14.jpg and the timed BRoll layer can be
  wired into the Remotion project against these same timecodes.
