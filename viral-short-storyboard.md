**The 4 traits every outlier shares, and every floor video breaks:**

1. **Line 1 is a personal-stakes impossibility**, not a metaphor and not a recap. The single best-performing hook on the channel (`Born Into Debt`, verbatim): *"Right now, a complete stranger is drowning in debt so that the money in your account can exist."* Compare the floor: *"You can't sell a fish that's still in the sea."*
2. **The topic is the viewer's money; the Islamic mechanism is the answer, never the subject.** Savings, mortgage, inflation, income = outliers. Gharar, murabaha, takaful = floor.
3. **No Arabic term in the hook.** `Born Into Debt` went further — no Arabic term in the *title* either, and it tied the channel record with zero series baggage. That is the strongest single signal in the dataset: **stop leading with the fiqh vocabulary.**
4. **Tight run-time, sting in the first 3 seconds.** `Born Into Debt` is 46s. The floor videos bury the payoff at 9–13s, past the swipe.

**Therefore the next short should be:** a universal money shock, second-person, no Arabic word in title or hook, ~50s, mechanism revealed visually, Islamic answer as the *resolution* in the last 10s. Highest-emotion topic they have not yet touched: **your bank does not have your money.** It is the direct sequel to two separate 1K+ outliers (savings trap, born into debt) and the most viscerally checkable claim in banking.
