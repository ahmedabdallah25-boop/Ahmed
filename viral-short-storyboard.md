**The 4 traits every outlier shares, and every floor video breaks:**

1. **Line 1 is a personal-stakes impossibility**, not a metaphor and not a recap. The single best-performing hook on the channel (`Born Into Debt`, verbatim): *"Right now, a complete stranger is drowning in debt so that the money in your account can exist."* Compare the floor: *"You can't sell a fish that's still in the sea."*
2. **The topic is the viewer's money; the Islamic mechanism is the answer, never the subject.** Savings, mortgage, inflation, income = outliers. Gharar, murabaha, takaful = floor.
3. **No Arabic term in the hook.** `Born Into Debt` went further — no Arabic term in the *title* either, and it tied the channel record with zero series baggage. That is the strongest single signal in the dataset: **stop leading with the fiqh vocabulary.**
4. **Tight run-time, sting in the first 3 seconds.** `Born Into Debt` is 46s. The floor videos bury the payoff at 9–13s, past the swipe.

**Therefore the next short should be:** a universal money shock, second-person, no Arabic word in title or hook, ~50s, mechanism revealed visually, Islamic answer as the *resolution* in the last 10s. Highest-emotion topic they have not yet touched: **your bank does not have your money.** It is the direct sequel to two separate 1K+ outliers (savings trap, born into debt) and the most viscerally checkable claim in banking.

## 3. Plates (both orientations)

**Rendered and committed:** `remotion/public/plates/{landscape,vertical}/` — 14 layered
PNGs each, generated by `npm run plates`. The vertical set is separately composed, not the
landscape set cropped. See that folder's README for the file → beat →
animation mapping. They're layer-separated (vault door, shutter, blueprints and coins are
their own transparent files) because the beat components animate those independently.

The prompts below are kept as the art direction of record — use them if you want to
replace any plate with an AI or 3D render. Anything you swap in must preserve the same
layer split, or the matching beat component won't animate.

Global style suffix — append to every prompt for series consistency:
`Horizontal 16:9 composition, cinematic 3D render, deep navy-to-black gradient background, single warm gold key light, volumetric haze, muted teal rim light, matte materials, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no watermarks, generous negative space in the right half for type, photoreal but slightly stylized, 8k detail.`

1. **Phone balance (beats 1–2)** — `A single modern smartphone standing upright in the left third of a wide dark space, screen glowing cold white-blue showing a minimal banking app transaction list, the right two thirds empty for type, faint reflection on a black glossy floor beneath it, gold light raking from the right.`
2. **Empty vault (beat 3)** — `A massive circular steel bank vault door centred in a wide frame, swung open on a pitch-black interior, one lone gold coin resting on the floor inside catching a single shaft of light, vast emptiness behind it, dust motes in the air, low camera angle looking up at the door.`
3. **Lending cascade (beat 4)** — `A waterfall of gold coins in the left half of a wide frame, splitting into smaller and smaller streams as it falls through five glass tiers, each tier narrower and catching less than the one above, the right half an empty dark navy void.`
4. **Thirty owners (beat 5)** — `A wide wall of thirty identical glowing phone screens in ten columns and three rows, all showing the same faint white glow, one single gold coin hovering in front of the grid centre, cold blue screens against a warm gold coin.`
5. **The run (beat 6)** — `Silhouetted crowd of people spread across the foreground in the rain outside a wide grand stone bank facade at dusk, a heavy steel shutter half-lowered over the entrance, warm light leaking from the closing gap, shot from behind the crowd, no faces visible.`
6. **Real assets (beat 7)** — `A full gold vault interior filling the left of a wide frame, coins piled to its mouth, and stacked down the right three glowing wireframe blueprints of real things: a house, a solar panel array, a delivery truck, gold particles arcing from the vault across to the blueprints.`

## 4. Motion graphics prompts

**Pipeline:** Remotion owns the timeline and the final render (it's the compositor). HyperFrames produces the four typographic/data overlays as transparent WebMs, dropped into the Remotion timeline with `<Video>` inside the matching `<Sequence>`. The six stills from §3 are the plates in `public/plates/`.

### Remotion (React/TSX — one `<Sequence>` per beat)

**Built, voiced and rendering in three cuts:** `remotion/` — `npm install && npm run vo &&
npm run render:all` produces `part15-landscape.mp4` (1920×1080, 64.3s),
`part15-vertical.mp4` (1080×1920, 64.3s) and `part15-short.mp4` (1080×1920, **46.7s** —
the Shorts read, tightened against the outlier profile in §1).

Beat boundaries are **measured from the voice track**, not from the timings in §2 — the
script estimated 52s and the read is 64.3s. See `remotion/README.md` for the retime
pipeline, the two orientations, the Sequence-local frame-numbering trap, and the
documented deviations from the specs below.

Root spec: `<Composition id="Part15" width={1920} height={1080} fps={30} durationInFrames={1560} />`. All motion from `useCurrentFrame()` + `interpolate`/`spring` — no CSS transitions or `requestAnimationFrame`. Every `interpolate` call uses `extrapolateLeft: 'clamp', extrapolateRight: 'clamp'`. Plates render via `<Img src={staticFile(...)}/>` wrapped in an absolute-fill div with `transform: scale()` for Ken Burns.

Prompt for each component (`src/beats/*.tsx`) — note these read in **absolute** frames; the components convert to Sequence-local:

- **`Beat1Balance.tsx` — frames 0–240 (beats 1–2)** — `Plate phone-balance.png (phone in the left third), scale interpolated 1.00 → 1.06 across the full 240 frames for a slow push-in. Overlay the HyperFrames reserve-counter WebM in a Sequence at frame 0. Add a 4px-amplitude handheld drift: translateX/Y driven by Math.sin(frame / 17) and Math.sin(frame / 23). Screen-glow div (radial-gradient, mix-blend-mode: screen) opacity interpolated 1 → 0.8 over frames 120–156 so the light drops as the number falls.`
- **`Beat3Vault.tsx` — frames 240–420** — `Plate empty-vault.png. Door layer as a separate transparent PNG with transformOrigin on the hinge edge, rotateY 0 → 90deg via spring({frame, fps, config: {damping: 12, mass: 2}}) for heavy inertia and a settle-bounce. Camera dolly = parent scale 1.0 → 1.25 over frames 285–420. Twenty dust-mote divs, each position seeded from its index with a deterministic random(index) helper, drifting upward at index-varied speeds.`
- **`Beat4Cascade.tsx` — frames 420–660** — `Plate lending-cascade.png held static, no camera move. Five tier layers, each emitting coins: coin i's Y = ((frame * speed + offset_i) % 1920), count per tier = [24, 18, 13, 9, 6] so each stream is visibly thinner than the one above. All positions are pure functions of frame — deterministic, so the render is frame-accurate and seekable. Layer the HyperFrames multiplier-chain WebM on top at frame 420.`
- **`Beat5Owners.tsx` — frames 660–900** — `Plate thirty-owners.png. Thirty absolutely-positioned screen-glow divs on a 5x6 grid, each fading in over 9 frames starting at frame 660 + index * 2.5 — a 30-item stagger completing in 75 frames. Background grid blurs via filter: blur() interpolated 0 → 6px over frames 750–900 while the centre coin layer stays unblurred and un-animated. HyperFrames ownership-tally WebM on top.`
- **`Beat6Run.tsx` — frames 900–1080** — `Plate bank-run.png, camera locked off — no scale, no translate. Rain as a tiling PNG strip scrolled by translateY(frame * 22 % tileHeight). Shutter layer translateY 0 → 33% of frame height, easing Easing.in(Easing.cubic), landing on frame 1050. On the slam, a full-frame black div goes opacity 0 → 1 over frames 1050–1056, and the light-leak layer cuts to 0 on the same frame.`
- **`Beat7Assets.tsx` — frames 1080–1380** — `Plate real-assets.png. Slow crane-up: translateY 0 → -80px across the beat. Three blueprint layers fill bottom-to-top using clipPath: inset() interpolated 100% → 0%, staggered 30 frames apart — house at 1110, panels at 1140, truck at 1170, each filling over 40 frames. Gold particle stream from the vault to each blueprint: 40 divs on a quadratic bezier path, t = (frame + index * 3) % 90 / 90.`
- **`Beat8Kicker.tsx` — frames 1380–1560** — `Holds the final frame of the Beat 7 plate at scale 1.25, darkened by a black overlay at opacity 0.55. HyperFrames kicker-card WebM on top from frame 1380. Logo lockup springs in at frame 1500, config {damping: 200}.`

Audio: `<Audio src={staticFile('vo.mp3')}/>` at the root, and set the beat boundaries above from the actual VO waveform once recorded — the frame numbers here are the script's timings, not the recording's.

## 5. Packaging (the floor videos regressed here — don't repeat it)

**Caveat on this section:** these rules were derived from the channel's *Shorts*
outliers. The piece is now 16:9 horizontal, where the swipe-decision mechanics that
produced them do not apply — a horizontal 52s video sits between formats. The title and
hook advice still holds (it is about the viewer's money, not about the aspect ratio); the
run-time and CTA advice was written for the Shorts feed.

- Title carries **zero** Arabic terms. Put "Wadiah / 100% reserve" in the description only.
- Description leads with the full 15-part series list **plus** the playlist URL.
- End CTA points **forward** (Part 16 tease), never back at a weak video.
- Sting in line 1, mechanism by 0:14, resolution by 0:36. The Shorts cut honours the
  "under 55s" rule at 46.7s; the 16:9 long-form cut deliberately does not.
