# Klarna Short — edit list, cut to the recorded voiceover

Every scene of [`klarna-scene-pack.txt`](klarna-scene-pack.txt) timed against the delivered VO
(`ElevenLabs_20260803T10_42_33`, 142.84s mono) and matched to the visuals in the CapCut cut.
These timings replace the pack's estimates — the pack guessed 3:02, the delivery is **2:22.8**.

## What changed against the pack

- **Runtime 2:22.8, not 3:02.** 39s shorter. Still inside the 133-180s band the pack notes
  produced four of this channel's five best videos, so the length is right — but every
  timecode in the pack is now wrong and this file supersedes them.
- **The picture is longer than the voice.** The cut holds 153s of stills against 142.8s of VO,
  before the 20.2s black tail. Retime to the table below and the two meet.
- **The VO breathes more than the script implies.** 30.7s of the 142.8s is pause. Several short
  lines land long because the delivery holds them — scene 07 *"Then you owe Klarna"* runs 3.0s
  of speech against a written length that predicts 1.3s. Cut to the pauses, not the word count.

## How these timings were derived

No transcription tool is reachable from this session, so the alignment is **computed, not
transcribed**: the VO was split at every pause ≥0.28s below −35dB, giving 68 speech segments,
and the 47 script lines were fitted across them to best match each line's written length.
Boundaries therefore land on real silences — but which line owns which segment is inferred.
**Nine rows are marked ⚠** where the fit is loose; check those against the audio first. The
other 38 sit within normal speaking pace.
