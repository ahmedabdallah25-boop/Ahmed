## What changed

- **Runtime 2:22.8, not 3:02** — 39s shorter, and still inside the 133-180s band the pack notes
  produced four of this channel's five best videos. The length is right; the pack's timecodes
  are not, and this file supersedes them.
- **The picture currently runs longer than the voice.** 153s of stills against 142.8s of speech,
  before the 20.2s black tail. Retiming pulls the cut in rather than stretching it.
- **The read breathes.** 30.7s of the 142.8s is pause. Short lines often land long because the
  delivery holds them — scene 07 *"Then you owe Klarna"* runs 3.0s of speech against a written
  length predicting 1.3s. Cut to the pauses, never to word count.

## How these timings were derived, and how far to trust them

No transcription tool is reachable from this session, so the alignment is **computed, not
transcribed.** The VO was split at every pause ≥0.28s below −35dB — 68 speech segments — and the
47 script lines fitted across them by written length. **Every boundary lands on a real silence**,
but which line owns which segment is inferred.

Confidence is highest where the pack's own pacing agrees: the closing five scenes resolve to
6.4s, 4.1s, 8.4s, 13.0s and 8.7s against the pack's 7/5/10/10/5. **13 rows are marked ⚠**
where a line's speech time diverges most from its written length — those are where a
segment may have landed on the wrong side of a boundary. Check them first; a 2-second listen
at each in-point settles it.
