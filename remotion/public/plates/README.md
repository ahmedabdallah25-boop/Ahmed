# Part 15 plates

14 PNGs generated by `remotion/scripts/make-plates.mjs` (`npm run plates` — deterministic,
safe to re-run). Full-frame at **1920×1080 landscape**, except two that Remotion uses at
natural size: `coin.png` is 96×96 and `shutter.png` is 380×380 (exactly the arched doorway
in `bank-run.png`). Sizes are declared in the generator's `sizes` map.

The layouts are composed for a wide frame rather than scaled from a vertical original —
the phone sits left of its balance figure, the lending cascade occupies the left half, and
the owner grid is 10 × 3 instead of 5 × 6.

These are **layered**, not flattened stills, because the beat components animate
foreground elements independently of their backgrounds. A single composite image per
beat would not be animatable.

## Notes

- Palette is defined once at the top of the generator: gold `#D4A24C`, teal `#3E8E8C`,
  navy `#0B1A2E`. Keep the HyperFrames overlays on the same three values.
- Geometry the beats also need (vault, arch, grid, tiers, rain period) is declared at the
  top of the generator and mirrored in `src/theme.ts` as `LAYOUT`. Nothing checks the two
  against each other, so change them together.
- Scatter (coin pile, rain streaks, crowd) goes through a seeded `mulberry32` PRNG.
  `i * k % n` was tried first and banded into visible diagonal stripes.
- No text is baked into any plate. All figures and type come from the HyperFrames
  overlays, so copy changes never require re-rendering a plate.
- To restyle, edit the generator rather than the PNGs — the layer split and the exact
  pixel geometry the beat components animate against are both encoded there.
