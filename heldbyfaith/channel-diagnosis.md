## The one-paragraph version

upload on the channel and it is the first thing a moved viewer is sent to.

## What is actually wrong

### 1. Both front doors point at the worst video on the channel

The channel description says:

```
If you are the one who was just diagnosed, or you are the one holding their hand, start here:
Part 1, the diagnosis: https://youtu.be/GFKRpV_mhWg
The whole journey in order: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLczfvNC3NVjo
```

`GFKRpV_mhWg` has **4 views** and is 16:9 on a channel whose audience arrives vertically. The
playlist opens on the same video. So a viewer who is moved enough by a Short to tap through —
the exact person this channel exists for — is handed its weakest asset twice.

The strongest asset is `X4z5Ij0SQgI`, *"I survived. He didn't. Nobody prepares you for that"*:
1,235 views and **35 likes, a 2.8% like rate**, the highest engagement on the channel. That is
where "start here" should point. This is one line of text and it is the highest-leverage change
available.

### 2. Conversion is the binding constraint, and it is 0.25%

**24,364 views → 61 subscribers.** One subscriber per 399 views. Reach is solved; the destination
is not. Everything in this section is a destination problem, and none of it needs a single new
video.

### 3. The playlist is not doing its job

`PLczfvNC3NVjo` — *"My Cancer Journey — Full Story"* — is the only playlist on the channel. It
opens on the 4-view landscape Part 1, and it reports **60 videos on a 34-video channel**, so it
holds 26 entries that are not current uploads. Worth opening before it is linked from anywhere
else. The 28 Shorts that carry the audience are not organised into anything at all.

### 4. Channel keywords are corrupted, not merely weak

The August fix was applied, but unquoted. YouTube's channel keyword field is space-separated and
only double quotes hold a phrase together, so the API now returns **30 single words**:

```
muslim · cancer · story · pancreatic · cancer · journey · whipple · surgery · recovery ·
cancer · diagnosis · story · islamic · reminder · tawakkul · sabr · faith · through ·
illness · cancer · support · held · by · faith · chronic · illness · muslim · dua · when · sick
```

`by`, `when` and `through` are indexed as keywords. The channel's own name is three separate
terms. `automation/heldbyfaith_packaging.py` quotes every multi-word term before writing, so
running it repairs this; **pasting the list into Studio by hand does not, unless the quotes go in
with it.**

### 5. `xmCDlKXU694` breaks the repo's own mandatory thumbnail rule

It is 4:45 — over YouTube's 180-second Shorts cap — so it publishes as long-form and its
thumbnail renders in 16:9 slots. CLAUDE.md requires a custom 1280×720 thumbnail for every upload
over 3 minutes. It has none, and it has 0 views. It is also the surgery, which is the most
important beat in the story.

### 6. There is no write path. All three secrets are missing.

`HBF_CLIENT_ID`, `HBF_CLIENT_SECRET` and `HBF_REFRESH_TOKEN` are **all unset** — confirmed today
by **"HELD BY FAITH - 1. Check setup"**, run
[34673924580](https://github.com/ahmedabdallah25-boop/Ahmed/actions/runs/34673924580), which

## The decision

Ordered by leverage. The first three touch no video and cost nothing.

**1. Repoint "start here" in the channel description** from `GFKRpV_mhWg` to
`https://youtu.be/X4z5Ij0SQgI`. One line. Every other sentence of the description stays exactly
as written — it is the owner's own account and it is good.

**2. Fix the channel keywords, with the quotes.** See the paste block below.

**3. Open the playlist.** Work out what the 26 extra entries are, then decide whether
`PLczfvNC3NVjo` should hold the 28 Shorts in narrative order or whether a second playlist should.
Either way it must not open on a 4-view landscape video.

**4. Hold the cadence at its current rate — do not raise it.** Re-read the reach trend once the
uploads now under 72 hours have settled. If the newer half drops past 25%, cut to two a day; the
monitor will say so.

**5. Give the 28 Shorts somewhere to go.** They are 28 separate moments with no next step. A
pinned comment or an end-of-video line pointing at the playlist costs nothing per upload and is
the only thing that turns a Short viewer into a channel viewer.

**6. Re-cut `xmCDlKXU694` vertical**, or give it the 1280×720 thumbnail CLAUDE.md requires. It is
the surgery and it has 0 views. Do not unlist it — it is Part 6 of six, and hiding it opens a
hole in the middle of the only ordered account of the diagnosis on the channel.

**7. Mint `HBF_REFRESH_TOKEN`** so items 1, 2 and future packaging stop being hand work. See
[To finish the wiring](#to-finish-the-wiring).

**Not on this list: making more Shorts about the diagnosis itself.** Three tellings of it exist
and the newest one is the only settled underperformer on the channel.

## Ready-to-apply fix pack

`heldbyfaith/packaging-fix.json` carries all of this machine-readably, and
`automation/heldbyfaith_packaging.py` applies it — **once the secrets exist.** Until then, the
two changes below are five minutes in Studio.

### Channel description (Studio → Customisation → Basic info)

Change exactly one line. Replace:

```
Part 1, the diagnosis: https://youtu.be/GFKRpV_mhWg
```

with:

```
https://youtu.be/X4z5Ij0SQgI
```

Leave every other character alone.

### Channel keywords (Studio → Settings → Channel → Basic info → Keywords)

Paste this **with the quotation marks**. Without them YouTube splits every phrase into single
words, which is exactly how the current field ended up as `held`, `by`, `faith`:

```
"muslim cancer story" "pancreatic cancer journey" "whipple surgery recovery"
"cancer diagnosis story" "faith through illness" "held by faith" "chronic illness muslim"
"dua when sick" "cancer support" "islamic reminder" tawakkul sabr
"cancer survivor story" "life after cancer" "muslim health journey"
```

### To finish the wiring

1. Google Cloud Console → APIs & Services → Credentials → create or reuse an OAuth client.
2. OAuth consent screen → **Test users** → add the Google account that owns HELD BY FAITH.
3. Open `automation/authorize.html` locally, sign in, and **pick HELD BY FAITH at the chooser.**
   A refresh token is bound to the channel selected there — one Google account owning several
   channels is not enough, and a token minted against the wrong one is a valid token that writes
   to the wrong channel.
4. Add three **repository** secrets — not Environment, not Dependabot:

   | Secret | Value |
   |---|---|
   | `HBF_CLIENT_ID` | the client ID |
   | `HBF_CLIENT_SECRET` | the client secret |
   | `HBF_REFRESH_TOKEN` | printed by step 3 |

   **Do not reuse `new1`/`new2`/`new3`.** Those drive every Finance % Decoded workflow.

5. Run **"HELD BY FAITH - 1. Check setup"** to confirm, then **"2. Fix packaging"** — dry run
   first, it is ticked by default.

Both write paths compare the authenticated channel against `expect_channel_id` and abort on a
mismatch, so a mis-minted token fails loudly rather than repackaging another channel. The
description write additionally refuses if the live text has drifted from the copy recorded in
`packaging-fix.json`, so a Studio edit made in the meantime is never overwritten. That guard is
pinned by `automation/test_heldbyfaith_channel_meta.py`.
