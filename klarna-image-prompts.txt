WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY

1. TOKEN COST. The Track A/B prompts repeat the same 60 words of style and
   costume in every single prompt. Here that is hoisted into two blocks you
   paste once. Each scene prompt is then ~45 words instead of ~115 — a little
   over half the tokens per generation, and the look holds better because the
   style string is byte-identical every time instead of paraphrased.

2. FULL SCRIPT COVERAGE. Five scenes (02, 05, 06, 07, 11) run 5.8–6.5s and
   carry TWO ideas each. One still could not cover both, so the late fee and
   "that's the actual product" had no frame at all — they were captions over an
   unrelated picture. Those five scenes now get two stills each, cut on the
   scene's own CAP 2 in-point. 11 scenes, 16 frames, every VO line pictured.

3. NO FRAME HOLDS PAST ~3.3s. A 6.53s static frame in a Short is a scroll.
   Longest hold here is 4.73s (the hook, which has to breathe) and 4.37s (the
   rule, deliberately locked). Everything else sits between 2.9s and 3.5s.

4. NEGATIVES ARE NOW PER FRAME.  v1 carried one 24-term negative block applied
   identically to all 16 stills. Three of those terms were actively fighting
   the shots they were pasted onto:

     - "clutter, busy background" contradicts F05A (dozens of tags down
       converging rails IS the composition) and F07A (the basket is specified
       overfull, past the rim). Suppressing density there suppresses the shot.
     - "facial features, eyes, mouth, nose, lips, eyebrows, face, portrait"
       is load-bearing on the five cast frames and on F06A's cropped hands,
       and is dead weight on the ten pure-object frames — where the useful
       negative is the opposite one, "people, hands, figures", to stop a stray
       human wandering into a still life.
     - "text, lettering, numbers" does nothing for F02B, whose entire subject
       is an absence pressed into dust. That frame needs object-level
       negatives (no fifth card, no floating shadow), not typographic ones.

   So the block is split: a universal spine that is genuinely true of all 16,
   plus a NEGATIVE line appended to each prompt targeting that frame's own
   failure mode. Net effect on tokens is roughly neutral — the spine got
   shorter by the same amount the frame lines added.

   Two additions worth calling out, because they protect decisions v1 made in
   prose but never enforced in the prompt:
     - F06B negates warm light, amber, golden grade explicitly. The doc says
       it is the one cold frame and that the temperature break is the sting.
       The style block asks for a warm amber accent on every frame. Without a
       negative overriding it, the style block wins and the sting is lost.
     - F10 negates full breakage, shatter and spiderweb cracking. "A fracture
       that stops halfway" is a hard instruction for image models, which
       default to dramatic destruction; the whole read of the frame is that
       the card is still standing.

HARD CONSTRAINT — THE EDIT MUST FINISH UNDER 60 SECONDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Measured on this channel's own catalogue (inventory run 30883075618, 2026-08-04),
every public Short past its 72-hour test, normalised to views/day:

  31–60s   n=1    median 89.8 v/day
  61–90s   n=3    median  6.9 v/day   <-- no survivors
  >90s     n=11   median 42.4 v/day

61–90s is the only bucket on the channel with nothing in it that worked. The cut
lands at 55.37s, which clears it by 4.63 seconds and no more. So: no end card, no
extended hook, no breathing room at the tail. If the edit grows, it must be
trimmed back under 60 — not allowed to drift to 62.

The prompt pack cites "slate rule: 30–60s" as the reason for this length. That
rule was retired on 2026-08-03 as unevidenced (see next-slate.md rule 5). The
length is right; the stated reason is stale. Do not re-derive anything from it.

The scene boundaries are untouched — beats.ts, the VO and the caption table all
still line up. The five new cuts happen inside scenes, so nothing upstream moves.
Nothing in this revision moves a boundary either: v2 changes negatives only.
