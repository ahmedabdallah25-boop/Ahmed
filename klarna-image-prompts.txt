WHY v2 EXISTS — three plates were test-generated and three things broke

Test: F01, F02A, F02B on Z Image, 9:16, 2026-08-04.

  ASKED                       GOT
  4 empty boxes on a screen   3
  4 cards in a row            6, reflowed into a 2x3 grid
  4 cards + 1 gap             9, reflowed into a 3x3 grid, two hollow

1. COUNTING. Exact counts above about three are unreliable in every diffusion
   model, and a horizontal row of four does not fit a 9:16 canvas — four cards
   side by side in a 1152x2048 frame would be postage stamps, so the model
   reflows them into a grid that fills the height. It did this twice,
   independently. Paying for a better model does not fix this.

   "Four payments" is the premise of the video. So the generator is now out of
   the counting business: scenes 01 and 02 generate ONE object each and the
   layout is built in Remotion, where four is exactly four.

2. THE STYLE BLOCK WAS IN THE WRONG PLACE. v1 hoisted it to the END of every
   prompt to save tokens. These models weight early tokens hardest, so it was
   the last thing read and the first thing dropped: across three frames there
   was no raking key, no long defined shadow, and the warm amber accent
   appeared zero times. The saving was real, the position was wrong. The look
   block now LEADS.

   What did survive was the no-text ban — because it was stated three ways in
   one prompt. Repetition beats position for hard constraints, so every prompt
   below still repeats it inline.

3. HEX CODES ARE IGNORED. `#E85F42` came back as grey sleeves. Prompts now use
   plain colour names; the hex values live in the edit reference below, which
   is the only place they were ever going to be honoured anyway.

One thing the test got right, and it was the biggest risk in the pack: NOT ONE
CHARACTER OF TEXT appeared in any of the three frames. The burn-in strategy is
safe.

And one accident worth keeping: asked for an empty gap, the model produced a
hollow card outline with the dark surface showing through the middle. That
reads as "missing" instantly — better than the dust impression v1 invented.
It is now plate P3.

WHAT ELSE CHANGED FROM v1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FULL SCRIPT COVERAGE (kept from v1). Five scenes run 5.8-6.5s and carry two
ideas each; one still could not cover both, so the late fee and "that's the
actual product" had no frame at all. Those five split on their own CAP 2
in-point, so the image change and the second line of type land together.
11 scenes, 16 frames, every VO line pictured. Scene boundaries are untouched —
the VO, the caption table and beats.ts all still line up.

NO FRAME HOLDS PAST ~3.3s (kept from v1), except the hook at 4.73s and the rule
at 4.37s, which are deliberate.

SAFE AREA — CORRECTED. This one is a genuine conflict inherited from the source
pack. Its stills are framed "subject in the lower two-thirds, clean headroom
above", but its caption spec puts CAP 1 at 62% of frame height and CAP 2 at 76%.
On a 1920-tall frame that is y1190-y1500 — the lower middle. The two
instructions collide, and the F01 test render landed the phone at 32-68%,
straight through the caption band.

  Correct safe area: keep the lit subject inside the top 20-58% of the frame.
  Hold 58-82% dark and quiet — that is where both caption lines sit. The bottom
  18% stays empty. Every prompt below is framed to this, not to v1's "headroom
  above", and the pushes are sized so the band stays clear at the end of the move.

HARD CONSTRAINT — THE EDIT MUST FINISH UNDER 60 SECONDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Measured on this channel's own catalogue (inventory run 30883075618, 2026-08-04),
every public Short past its 72-hour test, normalised to views/day:

  31-60s   n=1    median 89.8 v/day
  61-90s   n=3    median  6.9 v/day   <-- no survivors
  >90s     n=11   median 42.4 v/day

61-90s is the only bucket on the channel with nothing in it that worked. The cut
lands at 55.37s, clearing it by 4.63 seconds and no more. No end card, no
extended hook, no breathing room at the tail.

The source pack cites "slate rule: 30-60s" as the reason for this length. That
rule was retired on 2026-08-03 as unevidenced (next-slate.md rule 5). The length
is right; the stated reason is stale.
