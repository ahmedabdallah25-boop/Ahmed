WHY THIS ONE, AND WHY NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The source format is a SILENT ESCALATION LADDER: one transaction repeated six
  times, exactly one variable changing, no dialogue, on-screen text used as
  labels only. It did 12,189,544 views and a 2.0% like rate with zero narration.
- It is a genuinely new lane for this channel. channel-reset.md records that
  every subject family here collapses by entry 3 or 4 and the formula-perfect
  third inflation video did 43 views. The failure was never the explaining —
  it was that every video was the same video. This one has no VO, no host,
  no diagram.
- The source's engine is an INVERSION: as the money gets bigger, the thing you
  buy gets smaller. This pack keeps the inversion but moves the variable off
  nationality (which reads as punching down from a UK channel) and onto the
  payment method — where it becomes the channel's actual subject.
- The ladder does not end in absurdity like the source. It escalates to absurdity
  and then RESETS. That final panel is the only reason this is this channel's
  video and not a generic money meme.


WHAT MOVES, AND WHAT IS HELD CONSTANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HELD CONSTANT across all six panels:
  - the car (same model, same colour, same forecourt, same framing)
  - the buyer (same shirt, same hands, face never visible)
  - the camera grammar and the three-shot rhythm

THE SINGLE VARIABLE UNDER TEST:
  - the payment method, and the physical volume of money it costs

If the forecourt or the car visibly changes between panels, the argument
collapses on screen. See PRODUCTION ORDER, step 3.

BRAND-SAFETY NOTE — read before generating
================================================================================

Cars are UNBADGED. No grille logo, no wordmark, no recognisable silhouette of a
current production model. If a badge appears, regenerate — do not paint it out.

Money is GENERIC UNBRANDED PAPER. Plain rectangles, abstract guilloche pattern,
no numerals large enough to read, no national symbols, no portraits. Real
banknote lookalikes are both a legal problem and the single biggest AI-artefact
magnet in this pack.

No real lender, dealer, card network or retailer is named in any prompt, caption,
title, description or tag. The subject is the ARITHMETIC, which is fair comment.
The moment it is a named company it is a legal exposure and a slate that dates
within a month.

The title cards in the source video use a copyrighted third-party design and
narrator voice. This pack replaces them with an original card. Do not substitute
the original back in.
