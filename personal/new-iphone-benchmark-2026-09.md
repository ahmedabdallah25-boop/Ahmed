# Brand-new iPhone price benchmark: UAE stores, 27 September 2026

This compares du's shop price for each model against the cheapest price found at other UAE stores. All
prices are in AED, include VAT, and are for a new, sealed 256 GB phone unless marked otherwise.

**How the data was collected.** This session's network policy blocks every store site, so no price
here was read off a live page. Each one comes from a search engine's index of that store's page. They
can be days to weeks old. Treat each number as a benchmark, and confirm it at the counter.

The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max cost the same everywhere, because Apple sets one list price. Stores
compete on cashback, card offers, trade-in and instalment plans instead. Retailers also report that
launch stock sold out on pre-orders.

## 2. What the numbers say

**For the 17 Pro or 17 Pro Max, du is the most expensive option.** du still charges full launch price,
while other stores cut 500–600 before the iPhone 18 launch. Buy at Noon, Sharaf DG, Jumbo or Amazon, or
ask du to match the price.

**For the iPhone Air, du is the cheapest option.** 3,599 is about 700 below launch price.

**The 17 Pro Max at about 4,500 is the value pick against the 18 Pro Max at 5,499.** You save around
1,000 for a phone that is one year older.

**Apple raised prices on older models on 9 September.** It added US$100 to the iPhone 16, 17, Air and
17e on its own store. du's older price tags may still be the pre-rise ones, which is a reason to buy
those models at du soon rather than wait. Confirm the du price in store, because it may have been
updated since it was indexed.

## 3. At the counter

- **Buy the Middle East version with FaceTime.** Some Noon and Amazon listings are the "International
  Version", which can mean a different warranty path. Ask before you pay.
- **Buy from an authorised seller.** du, e&, Apple, Sharaf DG, Jumbo, iSTYLE and Virgin all qualify.
  Marketplace third-party sellers may not honour Apple's one-year UAE warranty.
- **Pay 0% instalments or use cashback instead of taking a telecom plan.** A du or e& plan (12 or 24
  months) bundles in a monthly contract, so compare its total cost with the outright price plus your
  current plan before you sign.
- **Take any price-match offer.** If du will not match 4,199 for the 17 Pro or about 4,500 for the 17
  Pro Max, buy elsewhere.
- **Trade in your old phone for credit.** Apple Trade In gives up to about 3,260 for an iPhone 13 or
  newer. Sharaf DG deducts trade-in value at checkout.
