# Used iPhone price benchmark: UAE, 27 September 2026

This compares du's shop prices for new phones against used and refurbished prices, so you can tell a
fair offer from an overpriced one before you pay. All prices are in AED, include VAT, and are for 256 GB
unless marked otherwise.

**How the data was collected.** This session's network policy blocked every UAE store (shop.du.ae,
amazon.ae, noon, revibe.me, refab.me, cartlow, dubizzle, pricena), so none of these prices were read
off a live page. Each one comes from a search engine's index of that store's page. They are usually a few
days to a few weeks old, and some Dubizzle figures are older than that. Treat each number as a
benchmark, and check the live price before you pay.

## 3. What the numbers say

**The best value right now is a certified-refurbished iPhone 16 Pro or 16 Pro Max.** At 2,770–3,000 you
pay about 30% less than du charges for the same phone new, and you still get a 12–18 month warranty.

**Classifieds are currently priced above certified refurb.** The Dubizzle 16 Pro at 3,150 costs more
than Amazon Renewed's 2,770, and it comes with no warranty. A private sale should sit 10–25% *below*
certified prices, not above them.

**Used 17-series phones are not worth buying yet.** A used 17 Pro Max at about 4,499 saves only 12% on
du's new price. A new 17 Pro has been sold for 4,199 during clearance, so buy new.

**Refurbished base iPhone 16s cost more than new ones.** du sells the iPhone 16 128 GB new for 2,849,
and some refurbished listings ask 3,199. At that gap, buy new.

**Your timing is good.** The iPhone 18 Pro launched nine days ago. Used prices usually fall most in the
weeks after a launch, when trade-ins flood the market, so an asking price should drift down through
October.

## 5. Checks before you pay

- **Battery health is 90% or higher.** A phone at 80–85% will soon need a battery, which costs AED 350
  or more. Settings → Battery → Battery Health.
- **Check the SIM version.** Several Amazon Renewed units are "Dual eSIM (US Version)" with no physical
  SIM tray. du supports eSIM, but if you want a physical SIM, choose the Middle East version (it also has
  FaceTime).
- **Check the parts history.** Settings → General → About → Parts and Service History should show no
  "Unknown Part" for the screen, battery or camera.
- **Make sure the phone is signed out of iCloud.** Activation Lock must be off before money changes
  hands. Have the seller erase the phone while you watch.
- **Check the warranty length.** Refab gives 18 months, while Amazon Renewed, Noon (when Noon is the
  seller), Revibe and Cartlow give 12 months. A private sale gives none.
