## 6. Packaging rules (non-negotiable, from the data)

- **Title:** universal money problem + stakes + specificity ("$282M", "Two Houses", "12 Minutes"). Never a fiqh term, never "Part N".
- **Thumbnail:** 3 elements max — one face/object, one number or arrow, ≤4 words. High contrast on dark background (niche convention that wins: FinancePhysio/Weird Finance style).
- **Hook (0–15s):** the paradox + what they lose by clicking away ("By minute 6 you'll know why your bank hopes you never watch this").
- **Structure:** open loop → mechanism → who profits → the halal/interest-free exit → one CTA (subscribe framed as "the only finance channel that shows you the no-interest route").
- **Retention target:** ≥50% average view duration at 8–12 min before scaling spend on a format.
