Notice what that means for this video, because it matters and it is easy to get wrong.

Reading the Quran in the order it was revealed is not a correction. Nobody here is saying the mushaf is out of order, or that it should be rearranged, or that the arrangement you have is a mistake somebody made. The arrangement is the Quran as it has been recited, memorised and prayed for fourteen hundred years, and on the majority view it is deliberate.

What the revelation order gives you is a second axis. Not a replacement for the first — a second one. The mushaf tells you what the book is. The chronology tells you how it arrived. Scholars have used both since the earliest period, because you cannot do tafsir without asking when a verse came and what it came in response to. That question has a name: asbab al-nuzul, the occasions of revelation. This video is that question asked across the whole book at once.
